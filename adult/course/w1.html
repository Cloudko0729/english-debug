📅 W1 — 自我介紹與公司角色

← 課程目錄 · 本週產出：30 秒自介＋email 開場結尾

🎯 本週結束時你要能：①用 30 秒英文介紹自己和公司 ②任何回信都能寫出自然的開場與結尾（不用再想）

⭐ 本週 5 個核心句（來自你的真實信件習慣）

1. Thank you for your email.
謝謝你的來信（萬用開場——你信裡最常用的就是它，繼續用）
收到任何信，第一句先謝，再說事情
2. This is Cloud from Fix-well Technology.
我是 Fix-well 的 Cloud（自報身分）
電話、email 第一次聯絡都用 This is，不用 I am Cloud（電話中尤其）
3. We are a repair service company for semiconductor test equipment.
我們是半導體測試設備的維修服務公司（公司一句話）
背熟這句，客戶來訪、自介、開會都是它
4. I will check the details and update you by Friday.
我會確認細節，最晚週五回覆你（承諾下一步＋期限）
「update you by＋時間」= 給對方安全感的關鍵句
5. Please let me know if you have any questions.
有任何問題請告訴我（標準結尾）
不要寫 If you have any question please tell me（中文直翻）

📧 Email 骨架（每封信都是這五層）

① Subject：講重點不寒暄 → RE: Power Supply Repair — Quotation
② 稱呼：Dear Mr. Lee, ／ Hi John,
③ 開場一句：Thank you for your email. ／ Thank you for your inquiry.
④ 正事：一段一件事，短句優先
⑤ 結尾：Please let me know if you have any questions. ＋ Best regards, Cloud

🎤 30 秒自介講稿（口說版）

Hello, my name is Cloud. I'm from Fix-well Technology in Hsinchu, Taiwan. We repair semiconductor test equipment — testers, probers, and power supplies. I handle repair cases and customer communication. If you have any equipment problems, please contact me anytime. Nice to meet you.

練法：先聽一次 → 跟讀 3 次 → 不看稿自己講一次並錄音。

📧 Email 常見錯誤語法

The repair item will send to customer verify.
will send 漏了被動（該用 be sent）；customer verify 是中文直翻，英文要用 for verification
The repaired unit will be sent to the customer for verification.
This problem maybe related with copy board.
maybe（副詞：也許）跟 may be（動詞片語：可能是）搞混；related with 應該是 related to
This issue may be related to the copied board.
Please help check this issue, thanks.
help check 是「幫我看一下」的直翻，正式信件裡太籠統，講清楚你要對方做什麼動作
Could you please check this issue and let us know the result?
We will arrange the shipment if test pass.
if 子句裡動詞要跟主詞一致，還漏了冠詞：the test passes
We will arrange the shipment once the test passes.
We are still checking, not confirm yet.
checking／confirm 詞性不一致；否定用法生硬，直接用「還沒被確認」的被動句更自然
We are still checking the root cause; it has not been confirmed yet.

🛠️ 維修紀錄撰寫範例

英文維修紀錄固定寫三層：Symptom（現象）→ Action（處置）→ Result（結果），每層一句就好，不用寫得複雜。

Symptom：The relay contact was unstable during the load test.
Action：Replaced the relay and re-ran the load test.
Result：The unit passed with stable output across all channels.
Symptom：DCCAL failed before the PPMU module was replaced.
Action：Replaced the PPMU module and repeated DCCAL.
Result：DCCAL passed; the unit was released for final verification.
Symptom：No power was present at the driver input.
Action：Traced the power net, found an open connection near the connector, and resoldered the joint.
Result：Power was restored and the unit passed the functional test.

📖 W1 週一 — 讀信＋圈句型

← 本週重點 · 讀一封修正版真實信，找出可重複使用的句型

先讀這封信（改編自真實維修回覆信，已去識別化）

Subject: RE: Repair Inquiry — DC Power Supply
Dear Mr. Lee,

Thank you for your email. This is Cloud from Fix-well Technology.
We are a repair service company for semiconductor test equipment.

We have received your inquiry about the DC power supply repair. I will check the details with our engineer and update you by Friday.

Please let me know if you have any questions.

Best regards,
Cloud

粗體就是本週 5 個核心句——它們在幾乎每封信都能重複使用。讀兩次再作答。

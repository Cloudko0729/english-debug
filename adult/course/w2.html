📅 W2 — 收到詢問的確認回覆

← 課程目錄 · 本週產出：收到詢問 30 秒內先安心對方＋一句確認你懂客戶要問什麼

🎯 本週結束時你要能：①收到客戶詢問信，馬上用一段回覆讓對方安心 ②用「To confirm, ... is that correct?」重述你聽懂的內容，避免會錯意

⭐ 本週 5 個核心句

1. Thank you for reaching out regarding the power supply issue.
謝謝你來信詢問電源供應器的問題（開場點出主題）
reach out regarding＋主題，比 Thank you for your email 更明確指出是什麼事
2. We have received your inquiry and will look into it right away.
我們收到你的詢問了，會馬上處理（確認收到＋馬上處理）
look into it＝著手查看，比 check it 正式一點，email 很常用
3. To confirm, you would like us to check the power supply for an intermittent output issue — is that correct?
確認一下，你是要我們檢查電源供應器的間歇性輸出問題，對嗎？（重述確認）
To confirm, ... — is that correct? 是萬用重述句型，換掉中間內容就能套用任何情境
4. We will get back to you with more details by tomorrow.
我們最晚明天會回覆你更詳細的內容（承諾下一步＋期限）
get back to you＝回覆你，比 reply you 自然（reply 是不及物動詞，不能直接接受詞）
5. Please let us know if there is any additional information we should know.
如果有其他我們該知道的資訊，請告訴我們（邀請補充資訊）
if there is any... 別漏掉 there，這是最常見的漏字錯誤

📧 Email 骨架（確認回覆版）

① Subject：RE: Inquiry — DC Power Supply Repair
② 稱呼：Dear Ms. Wang,
③ 開場一句：Thank you for reaching out regarding the power supply issue.
④ 確認理解：We have received your inquiry... To confirm, ... — is that correct?
⑤ 結尾：We will get back to you with more details by tomorrow. ＋ Please let us know if there is any additional information we should know. ＋ Best regards, Cloud

🎤 電話確認講稿（口說版）

Hi, this is Cloud from Fix-well Technology. Thank you for reaching out about the power supply issue. I just want to confirm — you're seeing an intermittent output problem on the DC power supply, is that right? Great, we'll look into it and get back to you with more details by tomorrow. Please let us know if there's anything else we should know.

練法：先聽一次 → 跟讀 3 次 → 不看稿自己講一次並錄音。

📧 Email 常見錯誤語法

We are already received your inquiry.
already 要跟完成式 have 搭配，不是跟 be 動詞：have already received
We have already received your inquiry.
To confirm you want check the power supply, right?
口語省略太多：漏了逗號停頓、漏了 us to（要別人做事用 want someone to do），正式信件要寫完整
To confirm, you would like us to check the power supply — is that correct?
We will reply you tomorrow.
reply 是不及物動詞，不能直接接人當受詞（reply you 錯），要嘛 reply to you，要嘛換動詞
We will get back to you tomorrow.
Please tell me if have any different information.
if 子句漏了主詞 there：if there is，different information 也不是「額外資訊」的意思，該用 additional
Please let us know if there is any additional information.
Thanks for your inquiry, we will checking it.
逗號接兩個完整句子是 run-on（該分兩句或用連接詞）；checking 前面漏了 be 動詞
Thank you for your inquiry. We will look into it.

🛠️ 維修紀錄撰寫範例

英文維修紀錄固定寫三層：Symptom（現象）→ Action（處置）→ Result（結果），每層一句就好。

Symptom：The connector pins showed poor contact during the retest.
Action：Cleaned the connector and reseated the cable.
Result：The contact test passed with no further abnormality.
Symptom：The thermal image showed an unusually hot spot near the regulator.
Action：Replaced the regulator and repeated the thermal check.
Result：The thermal image was normal after the replacement.
Symptom：The EEPROM data was inconsistent with the reference board.
Action：Rewrote the EEPROM after confirming the correct configuration.
Result：The board passed verification with the corrected data.

📖 W2 週一 — 讀信＋圈句型

← 本週重點 · 讀一封確認收到詢問的回覆信，找出可重複使用的句型

先讀這封信（改編自真實維修回覆信，已去識別化）

Subject: RE: Inquiry — DC Power Supply Repair
Dear Ms. Wang,

Thank you for reaching out regarding the power supply issue.
We have received your inquiry and will look into it right away. To confirm, you would like us to check the DC power supply for an intermittent output issue — is that correct?

We will get back to you with more details by tomorrow.
Please let us know if there is any additional information we should know.

Best regards,
Cloud

粗體就是本週 5 個核心句——它們在幾乎每封「收到詢問」的回信都能重複使用。讀兩次再作答。

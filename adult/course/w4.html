📅 W4 — 已收件將安排檢查

← 課程目錄 · 本週產出：收到設備後，一段信／電話就能讓客戶知道進度並安心等待

🎯 本週結束時你要能：①收到設備後馬上回信告知已收件＋預計檢查時間 ②若中途發現額外問題，能先告知客戶再繼續處理

⭐ 本週 5 個核心句

1. Thank you for sending the unit — we have received it and will arrange the inspection shortly.
謝謝你寄送設備——我們已經收到，會盡快安排檢查（開場＋確認收件）
received it，用 it 代替 the unit，避免同一句重複名詞
2. The incoming inspection is expected to take one to two business days.
進廠檢查預計需要一到兩個工作天（說明檢查所需時間）
incoming inspection＝進廠檢查／來料檢驗，維修流程固定用詞；is expected to take＋時間，是給預估時間的標準句型
3. We will share the findings with you as soon as the inspection is complete.
檢查完成後我們會盡快跟你分享結果（承諾下一步）
as soon as＋明確的完成點（inspection is complete），比籠統的 as soon as possible 更具體、更讓人安心
4. If we find any additional issues, we will inform you before proceeding with the repair.
如果我們發現任何額外問題，會在繼續維修前先告知你（先告知才繼續，避免爭議）
before proceeding with＋事情，是維修產業很重要的溝通習慣：先告知才動作，避免事後爭議費用或範圍
5. Thank you for your patience while we complete this process.
謝謝你在這段過程中的耐心等候（結尾致謝）
for your patience while＋過程，是任何耗時流程都能套用的結尾句

📧 Email 骨架（已收件版）

① Subject：RE: Unit Received — Inspection in Progress
② 稱呼：Dear Mr. Yeh,
③ 開場：Thank you for sending the unit — we have received it and will arrange the inspection shortly.
④ 正事：檢查所需時間 → 完成後會分享結果 → 若有額外問題會先告知
⑤ 結尾：Thank you for your patience while we complete this process. ＋ Best regards, Cloud

🎤 電話進度告知講稿（口說版）

Hi, this is Cloud from Fix-well Technology. Thank you for sending the unit — we've received it and will arrange the inspection shortly. The incoming inspection usually takes one to two business days. We'll share the findings with you as soon as it's complete, and if we find any additional issues, we'll let you know before proceeding. Thanks so much for your patience.

練法：先聽一次 → 跟讀 3 次 → 不看稿自己講一次並錄音。

📧 Email 常見錯誤語法

We receive the unit and will check it.
已經發生的動作要用現在完成式 have received，不是現在簡單式 receive
We have received the unit and will arrange the inspection shortly.
The inspection need one or two day.
need 少了第三人稱單數 s（needs）；day 應該是複數 days
The incoming inspection is expected to take one to two business days.
We will tell you result when inspection finish.
when 子句動詞要跟三單一致：finishes；result 前面漏了限定詞，改用 findings 更自然
We will share the findings with you as soon as the inspection is complete.
If we found more problem, we tell you before continue.
if 子句要用現在式 find；problem 該用複數 issues；before 後面接動名詞 continuing
If we find any additional issues, we will inform you before proceeding with the repair.
Thanks you patient for this process.
thanks you 應該是 thank you；patient 是形容詞，這裡要用名詞 patience
Thank you for your patience while we complete this process.

🛠️ 維修紀錄撰寫範例

英文維修紀錄固定寫三層：Symptom（現象）→ Action（處置）→ Result（結果），每層一句就好。

Symptom：The socket showed poor contact during the incoming inspection.
Action：Cleaned the socket and repeated the contact test.
Result：The unit passed the incoming inspection with no further issue.
Symptom：A missing component was found during IQC.
Action：Replaced the missing component before proceeding with the repair.
Result：The board passed the follow-up inspection.
Symptom：The cable showed intermittent contact during the initial check.
Action：Reseated the cable and repeated the check.
Result：The intermittent issue no longer appeared.

📖 W4 週一 — 讀信＋圈句型

← 本週重點 · 讀一封已收件通知信，找出可重複使用的句型

先讀這封信（改編自真實維修回覆信，已去識別化）

Subject: RE: Unit Received — Inspection in Progress
Dear Mr. Yeh,

Thank you for sending the unit — we have received it and will arrange the inspection shortly.

The incoming inspection is expected to take one to two business days. We will share the findings with you as soon as the inspection is complete. If we find any additional issues, we will inform you before proceeding with the repair.

Thank you for your patience while we complete this process.

Best regards,
Cloud

粗體就是本週 5 個核心句——它們在幾乎每封「已收件通知」的信都能重複使用。讀兩次再作答。

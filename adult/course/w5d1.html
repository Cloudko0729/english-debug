📖 W5 週一 — 讀信＋圈句型

← 本週重點 · 讀一封已收件通知信，找出可重複使用的句型

先讀這封信（改編自真實維修報價信，已去識別化）

Subject: Repair Quotation — Model TX-200, S/N 45120933
Dear Mr. Yeh,

Based on the inspection, we have prepared a quotation for the repair.

The total repair cost is USD 480, including parts and labor. The quotation is valid for 30 days from the date of this email. Please confirm in writing before we proceed with the repair.

If you prefer not to proceed, we can return the unit as it is.

Please let us know how you would like to proceed.

Best regards,
Cloud

粗體就是本週 5 個核心句——它們在幾乎每封「已收件通知」的信都能重複使用。讀兩次再作答。

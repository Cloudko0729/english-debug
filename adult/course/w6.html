📅 W6 — 議價與價格說明

← 課程目錄 · 本週產出：客戶嫌貴時，能拆給對方看費用組成、提替代方案，而不是直接降價或硬頂回去

🎯 本週結束時你要能：①用對方聽得懂的方式拆解費用組成 ②提出範圍縮小的替代方案 ③真的不能再降時，能說得得體不失禮

⭐ 本週 5 個核心句

1. I understand the cost is higher than expected — let me explain what it covers.
我理解費用比預期高——讓我說明它包含哪些項目（先接住情緒再講事情）
先承認對方的感受再解釋，比直接反駁有效得多。higher than expected 比 expensive 中性
2. The quotation covers three replacement parts and about four hours of labor.
報價包含三個更換料件與約四小時工時（拆解組成）
把數字拆開講，對方才能判斷貴在哪；covers 比 includes 更常用在報價說明
3. We can reduce the cost if we repair only the failing channel.
如果只維修故障的那個通道，費用可以降低（提替代方案）
降價的正確方式是縮小範圍，不是打折。這句同時保住價格與關係
4. Unfortunately, we are not able to lower the price further at this stage.
很抱歉，目前無法再降價（守住底線）
are not able to 比 cannot 委婉；at this stage 留下日後轉圜的餘地
5. Would you like us to quote the alternative option for comparison?
需要我們報另一個方案讓您比較嗎？（把球丟回去）
以問句結尾，讓對話繼續而不是停在拒絕上

📧 Email 骨架（議價與價格說明）

① Subject：RE: Repair Quotation — Cost Breakdown
② 稱呼：Dear Mr. Yeh,
③ 開場：I understand the cost is higher than expected — let me explain what it covers.
④ 正事：拆解費用組成 → 提出縮小範圍的替代方案 → 若不能再降，說明現況 → 詢問是否要報替代方案
⑤ 結尾：Please let me know which option works better for you. ＋ Best regards, Cloud

🎤 電話進度告知講稿（口說版）

Hi Mr. Yeh, this is Cloud from Fix-well Technology. I understand the cost is higher than you expected — let me explain what it covers. The quotation covers three replacement parts and about four hours of labor. If you'd like to bring the cost down, we can repair only the failing channel instead of all four. Unfortunately we're not able to lower the price further at this stage, but I'm happy to quote the alternative option so you can compare.

練法：先聽一次 → 跟讀 3 次 → 不看稿自己講一次並錄音。

📧 Email 常見錯誤語法

I know you feel expensive.
feel 後面不能直接接形容詞形容別的東西；expensive 形容的是價格不是人。改成中性的 higher than expected
I understand the cost is higher than expected — let me explain what it covers.
The price is include 3 parts and 4 hour labor.
is include 兩個動詞並排；hour 要複數 hours；報價說明用 covers 比 include 自然
The quotation covers three replacement parts and about four hours of labor.
We can more cheap if only repair one channel.
more cheap 不是比較級（cheaper）；這裡真正要說的是降低費用 reduce the cost；if 子句要有主詞
We can reduce the cost if we repair only the failing channel.
Sorry we cannot down the price.
down 不能當動詞用；cannot 直接拒絕太硬，用 are not able to＋at this stage 留餘地
Unfortunately, we are not able to lower the price further at this stage.
Do you want I quote other option?
want 後面接人要用 want us to；other option 少了限定詞，改 the alternative option
Would you like us to quote the alternative option for comparison?

🛠️ 維修紀錄撰寫範例

英文維修紀錄固定寫三層：Symptom（現象）→ Action（處置）→ Result（結果），每層一句就好。標準版之後附「精簡版」——真實工程師交班常用的電報式簡寫，不用完整文法，但夠清楚。

Symptom：Only one of the four channels failed the functional test.
Action：Quoted a partial repair covering the failing channel only.
Result：The customer accepted the partial repair and the work was scheduled.
精簡版：1 of 4 ch failed @ functional test → quoted partial repair, failing ch only → accepted, scheduled.
Symptom：The customer questioned the labor hours listed in the quotation.
Action：Broke the labor time down into inspection, replacement, and retest.
Result：The customer accepted the breakdown and approved the original quotation.
精簡版：Customer questioned labor hrs → broke down: inspect / replace / retest → accepted, original quote approved.
Symptom：The requested discount was below our minimum repair charge.
Action：Explained the minimum charge and offered a reduced repair scope instead.
Result：The customer selected the reduced scope and the case moved forward.
精簡版：Requested discount < min charge → explained min charge, offered reduced scope → customer took reduced scope.

📖 W6 週一 — 讀信＋圈句型

← 本週重點 · 讀一封已收件通知信，找出可重複使用的句型

先讀這封信（改編自真實議價回覆信，已去識別化）

Subject: RE: Repair Quotation — Cost Breakdown
Dear Mr. Yeh,

I understand the cost is higher than expected — let me explain what it covers.

The quotation covers three replacement parts and about four hours of labor. We can reduce the cost if we repair only the failing channel. Unfortunately, we are not able to lower the price further at this stage.

Would you like us to quote the alternative option for comparison?

Please let me know which option works better for you.

Best regards,
Cloud

粗體就是本週 5 個核心句——它們在幾乎每封「已收件通知」的信都能重複使用。讀兩次再作答。

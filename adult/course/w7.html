📅 W7 — RMA 申請與寄送

← 課程目錄 · 本週產出：開出 RMA、說清楚包裝與標示要求，讓設備能正確寄到而不出包

🎯 本週結束時你要能：①開立 RMA 編號並告知客戶 ②說明包裝與標示要求 ③收件後主動確認

⭐ 本週 5 個核心句

1. Please fill in the attached RMA form before shipping the unit back.
請先填寫附件的 RMA 表單再寄回設備（先填表再寄）
fill in / fill out 都可以，fill 後面不能直接接 the form。attached 表示附件在這封信裡
2. We have issued RMA number R-24081 for this case.
我們已為此案開立 RMA 編號 R-24081（給編號）
issue＝開立（編號、單據）。有編號對方才追得到，也是後續所有信的共同索引
3. Please mark the RMA number clearly on the outside of the package.
請將 RMA 編號清楚標示在外箱上（標示位置）
on the outside of the package 要講明白——標在裡面等於沒標，收貨端拆箱前看不到
4. The unit should be packed in its original box or equivalent protective packaging.
設備應以原廠外箱或同等防護包裝寄送（包裝要求）
or equivalent 是關鍵：原箱丟了也有替代方案，不會卡住對方
5. Once we receive the unit, we will confirm by email.
設備一到我們會以 email 確認（收件後主動回報）
Once＋完成點，比 after 更強調「一發生就」。主動確認能省掉對方一封追問信

📧 Email 骨架（RMA 申請與寄送）

① Subject：RMA R-24081 — Return Instructions
② 稱呼：Dear Mr. Yeh,
③ 開場：We have issued RMA number R-24081 for this case.
④ 正事：填寫附件表單 → 外箱清楚標示 RMA 編號 → 包裝要求 → 收到後我們會確認
⑤ 結尾：Please let us know the tracking number once the unit is shipped. ＋ Best regards, Cloud

🎤 電話進度告知講稿（口說版）

Hi Mr. Yeh, this is Cloud from Fix-well Technology. We've issued RMA number R dash two four zero eight one for this case. Before you ship the unit back, please fill in the RMA form I attached, and mark the RMA number clearly on the outside of the package. The unit should go in its original box, or equivalent protective packaging. Once we receive it, we'll confirm by email — and if you can send me the tracking number, I'll keep an eye out for it.

練法：先聽一次 → 跟讀 3 次 → 不看稿自己講一次並錄音。

📧 Email 常見錯誤語法

Please fill the RMA form before you ship back the unit.
fill 後面不能直接接表單，要用 fill in／fill out；ship back the unit 語序改成 shipping the unit back 更自然
Please fill in the attached RMA form before shipping the unit back.
We issue RMA number already.
已完成的動作要用現在完成式 have issued；already 位置錯了，而且沒給編號等於沒說
We have issued RMA number R-24081 for this case.
Please write RMA number on outside package.
RMA number 前要有限定詞 the；outside package 少了 the 和 of，正確是 on the outside of the package
Please mark the RMA number clearly on the outside of the package.
The unit must pack with original box.
設備是被包裝，要用被動 should be packed；用 in 不是 with；original box 前要有 its
The unit should be packed in its original box or equivalent protective packaging.
After we receive, we confirm you by mail.
receive 後面缺受詞；confirm 不接人；by mail 是實體郵件，email 要寫 by email
Once we receive the unit, we will confirm by email.

🛠️ 維修紀錄撰寫範例

英文維修紀錄固定寫三層：Symptom（現象）→ Action（處置）→ Result（結果），每層一句就好。標準版之後附「精簡版」——真實工程師交班常用的電報式簡寫，不用完整文法，但夠清楚。

Symptom：The unit arrived without an RMA number marked on the package.
Action：Matched the serial number against the open RMA list and logged the unit manually.
Result：The unit was accepted and the customer was asked to mark the number on future shipments.
精簡版：Unit arrived, no RMA# on box → matched S/N vs open RMA list, logged manually → accepted, customer reminded.
Symptom：One corner of the package was crushed on arrival.
Action：Photographed the packaging and checked the unit for shipping damage before opening the case.
Result：No damage was found on the unit and the inspection continued as planned.
精簡版：Box corner crushed on arrival → photographed packaging, checked for shipping damage → unit OK, inspection continued.
Symptom：The unit was shipped without any protective foam inside the box.
Action：Reported the packaging issue to the customer with photos taken at receiving.
Result：The customer agreed to use protective packaging for future shipments.
精簡版：No protective foam in box → reported to customer w/ receiving photos → customer agreed to pack properly next time.

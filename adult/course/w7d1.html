📖 W7 週一 — 讀信＋圈句型

← 本週重點 · 讀一封已收件通知信，找出可重複使用的句型

先讀這封信（改編自真實 RMA 通知信，已去識別化）

Subject: RMA R-24081 — Return Instructions
Dear Mr. Yeh,

We have issued RMA number R-24081 for this case. Please fill in the attached RMA form before shipping the unit back.

Please mark the RMA number clearly on the outside of the package. The unit should be packed in its original box or equivalent protective packaging.

Once we receive the unit, we will confirm by email.

Please let us know the tracking number once the unit is shipped.

Best regards,
Cloud

粗體就是本週 5 個核心句——它們在幾乎每封「已收件通知」的信都能重複使用。讀兩次再作答。

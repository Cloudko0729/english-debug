📅 W8 — 交期說明（ETA / lead time）

← 課程目錄 · 本週產出：把預計完成時間、料件到貨的影響講清楚，延遲時能主動更新而不是等對方追

🎯 本週結束時你要能：①給出明確的預計完成日 ②說明料件交期如何影響進度 ③日期有變時主動更新

⭐ 本週 5 個核心句

1. The estimated completion date is August 15.
預計完成日為 8 月 15 日（給明確日期）
estimated completion date 是交期溝通的核心名詞。給日期不要給「大概兩三週」，對方沒辦法排程
2. The replacement part has a lead time of about two weeks.
更換料件的交期約兩週（說明卡在哪）
lead time＝從下單到到貨的時間，是採購與維修共通的字。has a lead time of＋期間
3. We will start the repair as soon as the part arrives.
料件一到我們就會開始維修（承諾動作）
as soon as＋明確事件，讓對方知道你沒有在拖，只是在等料
4. The schedule may change if the part is delayed by the supplier.
若料件因供應商延遲，時程可能會變動（先講風險）
先講風險比事後解釋有效得多。用 may change 而不是 will change，保留彈性
5. We will update you if the estimated date changes.
預計日期若有變動我們會主動通知您（承諾主動回報）
這句是整封信最重要的承諾：主動更新。做到了，客戶就不會每兩天來追一次

📧 Email 骨架（交期說明（ETA / lead time））

① Subject：RMA R-24081 — Estimated Completion Date
② 稱呼：Dear Mr. Yeh,
③ 開場：The estimated completion date is August 15.
④ 正事：料件交期說明 → 料到就開工 → 供應商延遲的風險 → 日期有變會主動更新
⑤ 結尾：Thank you for your patience while we wait for the part. ＋ Best regards, Cloud

🎤 電話進度告知講稿（口說版）

Hi Mr. Yeh, this is Cloud from Fix-well Technology. I'm calling with an update on RMA R dash two four zero eight one. The estimated completion date is August fifteenth. The replacement part has a lead time of about two weeks, and we'll start the repair as soon as it arrives. The schedule may change if the supplier delays the shipment — but if the estimated date changes, I'll update you right away.

練法：先聽一次 → 跟讀 3 次 → 不看稿自己講一次並錄音。

📧 Email 常見錯誤語法

The finish date estimate is August 15.
finish date estimate 是中文語序直翻；英文的固定說法是 estimated completion date
The estimated completion date is August 15.
The part need about 2 week to arrive.
need 少了三單 s；week 要複數 weeks；交期有專門說法 has a lead time of
The replacement part has a lead time of about two weeks.
We start repair after part arrive.
start 要用未來式 will start；part 前要有限定詞；時間子句動詞要三單 arrives
We will start the repair as soon as the part arrives.
If supplier delay, maybe schedule will change.
supplier 前要有限定詞；delay 要三單且這裡料件是被延遲，用被動 is delayed；maybe 和 will 語意衝突，用 may 就好
The schedule may change if the part is delayed by the supplier.
I will tell you when the date change.
date 是第三人稱單數要用 changes；when 表示一定會變，用 if 較準確；tell you 改 update you 更專業
We will update you if the estimated date changes.

🛠️ 維修紀錄撰寫範例

英文維修紀錄固定寫三層：Symptom（現象）→ Action（處置）→ Result（結果），每層一句就好。標準版之後附「精簡版」——真實工程師交班常用的電報式簡寫，不用完整文法，但夠清楚。

Symptom：The replacement relay was not in stock when the repair was scheduled.
Action：Placed an order and informed the customer of the two-week lead time.
Result：The relay arrived on schedule and the repair was completed the same week.
精簡版：Relay out of stock @ scheduling → ordered, told customer 2-wk lead time → arrived on time, repair done same week.
Symptom：The supplier delayed the power module shipment by one week.
Action：Informed the customer of the revised estimated completion date.
Result：The unit was completed one week later than originally planned.
精簡版：Supplier delayed power module 1 wk → informed customer, revised ETA → completed 1 wk later than planned.
Symptom：The repair was finished ahead of the estimated completion date.
Action：Notified the customer and arranged an earlier shipment.
Result：The unit was returned three days ahead of schedule.
精簡版：Repair finished ahead of ETA → notified customer, arranged earlier shipment → returned 3 days early.

📖 W8 週一 — 讀信＋圈句型

← 本週重點 · 讀一封已收件通知信，找出可重複使用的句型

先讀這封信（改編自真實交期通知信，已去識別化）

Subject: RMA R-24081 — Estimated Completion Date
Dear Mr. Yeh,

The estimated completion date is August 15. The replacement part has a lead time of about two weeks.

We will start the repair as soon as the part arrives. The schedule may change if the part is delayed by the supplier. We will update you if the estimated date changes.

Thank you for your patience while we wait for the part.

Best regards,
Cloud

粗體就是本週 5 個核心句——它們在幾乎每封「已收件通知」的信都能重複使用。讀兩次再作答。

確認料號 / 規格 間歇性問題描述 複習單字卡

Part 1：今日 3 個核心句型

1. Could you confirm the exact part number and model version?
中文意思：請確認正確的料號和型號版本。
適用場景：要採購替代料或確認規格時，請對方提供確切資訊。
注意：exact 加上去語氣更精準，表示不要給大概的，要確切的數字。
2. The issue occurs intermittently when the unit powers on.
中文意思：這個問題在設備開機時偶爾會發生。
適用場景：向客戶或原廠描述偶發性問題的觸發時機。
注意：intermittently（偶發地）比 sometimes 更技術性；when 後面要加完整子句。
3. To avoid using the wrong replacement part, we need to verify the connector specification.
中文意思：為了避免使用錯誤的替代料，我們需要確認連接器規格。
適用場景：解釋為什麼要先確認規格再動作，有說服力的工程師語氣。
注意：To avoid... 開頭說明目的，邏輯清楚；replacement part 比 substitute 更常見。

Part 2：中翻英練習

先自己翻，再展開答案對照。

  1. 請確認正確的料號和型號版本。
  2. 這個問題在設備開機後偶爾會發生。
  3. 為了避免使用錯誤的替代料，我們需要確認連接器規格。
  4. 請提供標籤上的完整型號和序號照片。
  5. 目前問題不是每次都能重現，但在高溫環境下比較容易發生。
展開答案
  1. Could you confirm the exact part number and model version?
  2. The issue occurs intermittently after the unit powers on.
  3. To avoid using the wrong replacement part, we need to verify the connector specification.
  4. Could you provide a photo of the full model number and serial number on the label?
  5. The issue cannot be reproduced every time, but it is more likely to occur in a high-temperature environment.

Part 3：口說反應練習

直接用英文回答，不要停太久：

情境 1 — 客戶問需要確認什麼資訊
They say: "What information do you need from us?"
Reference: We need to confirm the exact part number, model version, and serial number before selecting the replacement part.
情境 2 — 主管問問題是固定還是偶發
They say: "Does this issue happen every time, or is it intermittent?"
Reference: It occurs intermittently. Based on the current information, it happens more often after the unit has been running for several hours.
情境 3 — 供應商問為什麼不直接出替代料
They say: "Why can't we just ship the substitute part right away?"
Reference: We need to verify the specification first to avoid compatibility issues with the customer's unit.

Part 4：今日英文 Bug

Bug 001 — "confirm us the part number" 結構錯誤

原句： Please confirm us the part number.

問題： confirm 不能直接接 us 當間接受詞，需要加介系詞 with，或直接省略人稱。

正確寫法：
Please confirm the part number with us.
Could you confirm the part number?（最簡潔）

套用： confirm [事項] with us，或直接 confirm [事項]，不要插入 us 在中間。

Bug 002 — "happens sometimes when power on" 多處問題

原句： The issue happens sometimes when power on.

問題①： happens sometimes → occurs intermittently（技術語境更精準）
問題②： when power on → when 後面要接完整子句：when the unit powers on

正確寫法：
The issue occurs intermittently when the unit powers on.

套用： 技術描述偶發問題一律用 occurs intermittently；when 子句要有主詞。

Part 5：今天只要記住這三句

  1. Could you confirm the exact part number and model version?
  2. The issue occurs intermittently when the unit powers on.
  3. To avoid using the wrong replacement part, we need to verify the connector specification.

道歉 + 更新 ETA Email Subject Line 複習單字卡 Day 4–7

Part 1：今日 3 個核心句型

1. Sorry for the delayed update. We are still waiting for the vendor's confirmation and expect to update you by tomorrow afternoon.
中文意思：抱歉更新延遲。我們仍在等待供應商確認，預計明天下午回覆您。
適用場景：沒能準時給對方消息時，先道歉再說明現況和下次更新時間。
注意：Sorry for the delayed update（加 the）比 Sorry for late update 更正式自然。
2. The delay was due to the replacement part not arriving yet. The current ETA is June 11.
中文意思：延遲是因為替換零件尚未到貨，目前預計到貨時間是 6 月 11 日。
適用場景：說明延誤原因並給出新的時程，有具體日期比「盡快」更專業。
注意：The delay was due to... 說明原因；The current ETA is... 給出新時程，兩句搭配使用。
3. Subject: [ETA Update] RMA-24581 - Replacement Board Shipment
中文意思：主旨：【交期更新】RMA-24581 替換板出貨
適用場景：讓收件者一眼看懂 email 是什麼事、什麼動作，不用打開才知道。
注意：格式：[狀態/動作] + 識別碼（RMA / 型號 / 料號）+ 簡短說明。
[ETA Update] RMA-24581 - Replacement Board Shipment
[Status Update] Model X200 - Test Result Pending
[Action Required] PN 88-2041A - Spec Confirmation Needed
[Follow-Up] SN 12345 - Awaiting Test Results

Part 2：中翻英練習

先自己翻，再展開答案對照。

  1. 抱歉更新延遲。我們仍在等待供應商的確認，預計明天下午回覆您。
  2. 延遲是因為替換零件尚未到貨。目前預計到貨時間是 6 月 11 日。
  3. 請使用這個主旨：RMA-24581 替換板出貨時間更新。
  4. 抱歉造成不便。我們會在收到測試結果後立即更新您。
  5. 目前維修仍在等待零件，新的預計完成時間是本週五。
展開答案
  1. Sorry for the delayed update. We are still waiting for the vendor's confirmation and expect to update you by tomorrow afternoon.
  2. The delay was due to the replacement part not arriving yet. The current ETA is June 11.
  3. Please use this subject: [ETA Update] RMA-24581 - Replacement Board Shipment.
  4. We apologize for the inconvenience. We will update you as soon as we receive the test result.
  5. The repair is currently waiting for parts. The new estimated completion time is this Friday.

Part 3：口說反應練習

直接用英文回答，不要停太久：

情境 1 — 客戶問維修為何比預期久
They say: "Why is the repair taking longer than expected?"
Reference: The delay was due to the replacement part not arriving yet. The current ETA is this Friday, and we will update you once the repair is completed.
情境 2 — Vendor 問是否還需要緊急出貨
They say: "Do you still need this part urgently?"
Reference: Yes, this is still urgent. Could you please confirm the shipment schedule and provide the latest ETA?
情境 3 — 主管問有沒有更新客戶
They say: "Did you update the customer?"
Reference: Yes. I apologized for the delayed update and informed them that the current ETA is June 11.

Part 4：今日英文 Bug

Bug 001 — "Sorry for late update" 缺冠詞

原句： Sorry for late update.

問題： late update 前面缺少定冠詞 the；另外 delayed 比 late 在商業 email 更正式。

正確寫法：
Sorry for the delayed update.
Apologies for the late response.

套用： 道歉 + 名詞前面要有 the：the delay、the inconvenience、the delayed update。

Bug 002 — "need more two days" 順序錯誤

原句： We need more two days.

問題： 數量要放在 more 前面，不是後面。

正確寫法：
We need two more days.
We need one more week to complete the repair.

套用： [數字] more [單位] — 固定順序：two more days / one more week / three more units。

Part 5：今天只要記住這三句

  1. Sorry for the delayed update. We expect to update you by [date/time].
  2. The delay was due to [reason]. The current ETA is [date].
  3. Subject: [ETA Update] RMA-XXXXX - [Brief Description]

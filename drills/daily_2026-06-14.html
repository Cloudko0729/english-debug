保固外維修報價 quote / approve / proceed 複習單字卡 Day 5–8

Part 1：今日 3 個核心句型

1. This unit is out of warranty, so a repair charge will apply.
中文意思：這台設備已超過保固期，因此會產生維修費用。
適用場景：客戶詢問為什麼這次維修需要付費。
注意：不要寫 need pay money（太口語）；a repair charge will apply 才是業界慣用說法。
2. We will provide a quotation before proceeding with the repair.
中文意思：我們會先提供報價，再進行維修。
適用場景：還沒開始付費維修前，先讓客戶知道流程。
注意：proceed with the repair 是固定搭配，不能寫 proceed the repair。
3. No repair work will be performed until we receive your approval.
中文意思：在收到您的同意前，我們不會執行任何維修工作。
適用場景：報價信結尾，避免客戶誤以為你已開始收費維修。
注意：語氣清楚但不失禮，適合正式 email 收尾。

Part 2：中翻英練習

先自己翻，再展開答案對照。

  1. 這台設備已超過保固期，因此會產生維修費用。
  2. 我們會先提供報價，再進行維修。
  3. 報價包含檢測、更換零件、人工與最終測試。
  4. 請確認是否同意此維修費用，讓我們可以繼續處理。
  5. 如果您不同意報價，我們可以將設備原狀退回。
展開答案
  1. This unit is out of warranty, so a repair charge will apply.
  2. We will provide a quotation before proceeding with the repair.
  3. The repair charge includes inspection, replacement parts, labor, and final testing.
  4. Please confirm your approval of the repair charge so we can continue the repair process.
  5. If the quotation is not approved, we can return the unit as-is.

Part 3：口說反應練習

直接用英文回答，不要停太久：

情境 1 — 客戶問為什麼這次 RMA 要收費
They say: "Why do we need to pay for this repair?"
Reference: This unit is out of warranty, so a repair charge will apply. We will provide a quotation before proceeding with the repair.
情境 2 — 客戶擔心你們還沒報價就開始修
They say: "Have you already started the repair?"
Reference: No repair work will be performed until we receive your approval. We will wait for your confirmation first.
情境 3 — 客戶說報價太高，問能否不修直接退回
They say: "The quotation is too high. Can you return the unit without repair?"
Reference: Yes, if the quotation is not approved, we can return the unit as-is. Please confirm how you would like us to proceed.

Part 4：今日英文 Bug

Bug 001 — "over warranty" / "pay repair money" 不專業

原句： This unit over warranty, so you need pay repair money.

問題： over warranty 不自然；pay repair money 太口語且不專業。

正確寫法：
This unit is out of warranty, so a repair charge will apply.
The warranty period has expired, so this repair is chargeable.

套用： 說明保固外維修、客戶需要付費時使用：out of warranty / a repair charge will apply / chargeable。

Bug 002 — "agree this quotation" 缺介系詞

原句： We will repair after you agree this quotation.

問題： agree 後缺介系詞；流程推進用 proceed with 才自然。

正確寫法：
We will proceed with the repair after we receive your approval.
Once you approve the quotation, we will arrange the repair accordingly.

套用： approve the quotation（同意報價）/ proceed with the repair（進行維修）。

Part 5：今天只要記住這三句

  1. This unit is out of warranty, so a repair charge will apply.
  2. We will provide a quotation before proceeding with the repair.
  3. No repair work will be performed until we receive your approval.

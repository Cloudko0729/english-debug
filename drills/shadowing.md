2. The root cause has **not** been confirmed **yet**. We are still investigating.
3. No abnormal behavior was observed **during** the test.

## 請求與確認

4. Could you **please** confirm the estimated delivery date?
5. We will proceed **once** we receive your confirmation.
6. Please **let us know** if you need any further information.

## 技術說明

7. This issue **may be related to** the EEPROM data mismatch.
8. The anomaly **appears** only under specific conditions.
9. We need to **compare** the golden file one more time.

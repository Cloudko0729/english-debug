# Repair Email Examples
> 真實工作場景的維修 Email 草稿，每個範例提供中文草稿、自然英文版、正式 email 版。

---

## 1. 送修通知給客戶

### 中文草稿
您好，我們已收到您的板子，序號 SN12345。目前已登記進我們的維修系統，預計 3-5 個工作天完成診斷，有結果我們會再通知您。

### 自然英文版
Hi [Customer],

Just letting you know we received the board (SN: 12345) today. We've logged it into our repair system. Should have a diagnosis within 3–5 business days — we'll reach out once we know more.

Thanks,
[Your name]

### 正式 email 版
Dear [Customer Name],

This is to confirm receipt of the unit (Serial Number: 12345) at our facility. The item has been registered in our repair tracking system.

We expect to complete the initial diagnosis within 3–5 business days and will provide an update accordingly.

Please feel free to contact us if you have any questions.

Best regards,
[Your name]
[Title / Company]

---

## 2. 維修進度更新

### 中文草稿
您好，跟您更新一下維修進度。我們已完成初步診斷，確認是 EEPROM 資料損毀造成的問題。目前正在燒錄 golden file，預計明天可以完成測試。

### 自然英文版
Hi [Customer],

Quick update on the repair — we've finished the initial diagnosis. Turned out the EEPROM data was corrupted, which caused the failure. We're reflashing it with the golden file now. Should be done with testing by tomorrow.

Will keep you posted.

[Your name]

### 正式 email 版
Dear [Customer Name],

We would like to provide an update on the repair status of your unit (SN: 12345).

Our diagnosis has identified the root cause as EEPROM data corruption. We are currently restoring the data using the golden file and expect to complete the verification testing by [date].

We will notify you once testing is confirmed successful.

Best regards,
[Your name]

---

## 3. 測試結果回覆

### 中文草稿
您好，板子測試已完成。所有功能測試 pass，checksum 比對也正常，確認可以出貨。請確認您的收件地址，我們準備安排出貨。

### 自然英文版
Hi [Customer],

Good news — the board passed all functional tests and the checksum looks good. It's ready to ship. Can you confirm your shipping address so we can get it out to you?

Thanks,
[Your name]

### 正式 email 版
Dear [Customer Name],

We are pleased to inform you that the repair of your unit (SN: 12345) has been completed successfully.

All functional tests have passed, and the checksum verification is confirmed correct. The unit is now ready for shipment.

Kindly confirm your current shipping address so we may proceed with the delivery arrangement.

Best regards,
[Your name]

---

## 4. 請客戶提供更多資訊

### 中文草稿
您好，我們在診斷過程中遇到一個問題。這個板子的錯誤只在特定條件下出現，無法在我們這邊穩定重現。能否請您提供更多資訊？例如：板子的使用環境、接的是哪個型號的模組、還有出問題時是否有異常的 log？

### 自然英文版
Hi [Customer],

We're having some trouble reproducing the failure on our end — it seems like it only shows up under specific conditions. Would you be able to share a bit more info? Things like:
- What environment the board was running in
- Which module it was connected to
- Any logs or error messages from when it failed

That'll help us narrow it down faster.

Thanks,
[Your name]

### 正式 email 版
Dear [Customer Name],

During our diagnostic process for the unit (SN: 12345), we have been unable to consistently reproduce the reported failure under standard test conditions.

To help us better identify the root cause, we kindly request the following information:

1. The operating environment and conditions under which the failure occurred
2. The module model and configuration connected at the time of failure
3. Any available error logs or abnormal indicators observed during the failure event

Your assistance in providing these details will significantly help accelerate the repair process.

Best regards,
[Your name]

## 5. 出貨確認通知

### 中文草稿
您好，板子已於今天出貨，快遞單號是 [tracking number]，預計 2-3 天到貨。麻煩您收到後確認一下，沒問題的話回覆我們就好。

### 自然英文版
Hi [Customer],

The board shipped out today. Tracking number is [tracking number] — should arrive in 2–3 days. Once you receive it, just give it a quick check and let us know if everything looks good.

Thanks,
[Your name]

### 正式 email 版
Dear [Customer Name],

We are writing to confirm that the unit (SN: 12345) has been shipped today via [courier name].

Tracking Number: [tracking number]
Estimated Delivery: 2–3 business days

Please inspect the unit upon receipt and confirm that it has arrived in satisfactory condition. Do not hesitate to contact us if you have any concerns.

Best regards,
[Your name]

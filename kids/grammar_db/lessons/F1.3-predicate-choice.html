STEP ①

核心例句

I am tired.
He is a teacher.
The book is on the desk.
She plays soccer.
STEP ②

比一比：哪句對？

She is plays soccer.
She plays soccer.
一般現在式的 plays 已構成述語，不再加 is。
I happy today.
I am happy today.
happy 是形容詞，需要 be 形成述語。
📌 講身分／心情／地點用 be；講動作用一般動詞
be + noun/adjective/place OR action verb
🟠 句型少了零件「我喜歡這首歌。」
❌ I am like this song. → ✅ I like this song.
like 在這裡是動詞，不是形容詞。
🟠 句型少了零件「他在教室裡。」
❌ He in the classroom. → ✅ He is in the classroom.
位置片語前需要 is。

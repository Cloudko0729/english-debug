STEP ①

核心例句

Are you tired?
Is she at home?
I am not busy.
They aren't ready.
STEP ②

比一比：哪句對？

Do you tired?
Are you tired?
tired 是形容詞，問句使用 be 路線。
She not is here.
She is not here.
not 放在 is 後面。
📌 問句把 be 搬到最前面；否定在 be 後面加 not
Be + subject ...?; subject + be + not ...
🟠 句型少了零件「他是老師嗎？」
❌ Does he a teacher? → ✅ Is he a teacher?
身分句使用 is 形成問句。
🟠 句型少了零件「我們不冷。」
❌ We not cold. → ✅ We are not cold.
否定狀態句仍需要 are。

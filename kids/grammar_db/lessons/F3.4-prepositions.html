STEP ①

核心例句

The keys are in the box.
The picture is on the wall.
We meet at seven.
I have music class on Monday.
STEP ②

比一比：哪句對？

We meet in seven.
We meet at seven.
精確時間點使用 at。
I have PE at Monday.
I have PE on Monday.
星期與日期通常使用 on。
📌 in／on／at 各自對應不同大小的地點和時間範圍
in/on/at and spatial relations
🟠 句型少了零件「我七月生日。」
❌ My birthday is on July. → ✅ My birthday is in July.
月份使用 in。
🟠 句型少了零件「貓在桌子下面。」
❌ The cat is in the table. → ✅ The cat is under the table.
under 表示下方，不是裡面。

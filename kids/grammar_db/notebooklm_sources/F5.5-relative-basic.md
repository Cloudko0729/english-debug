## 這堂課要教什麼

用 who、which、that、where 把必要資訊連到正確的人、物或地點。

句型公式：**noun + relative word + clause**

今天學完，小朋友要能自己造一句像這樣的話：
「The girl who won the race is my sister.」

## 情境對話（可以直接當開場小劇場）

A: Who is the boy who is holding the map?
B: He is Leo, the student who found the key.
A: Is that the key that opens the box?
B: Yes, it is the key that we need.

請用這段對話當開場，營造真實生活情境，再帶出文法規則。

## 自然例句

1. The girl who won the race is my sister.
2. This is the book that I borrowed.
3. We built a machine which saves water.
4. The park where we play is nearby.

## 常見錯誤對照（請用「你猜猜看哪句對」的口吻帶過）

- ❌ The girl which won the race is my sister. → ✅ The girl who won the race is my sister.
  （指人時使用 who。）
- ❌ This is the book who I borrowed. → ✅ This is the book that I borrowed.
  （指物時使用 that 或 which。）

## 中文母語者最容易犯的錯（請特別強調，因為是中文直翻習慣造成的）

1. 「那位幫我的老師很親切。」常被說成 ❌ The teacher which helped me is kind.
   正確：✅ The teacher who helped me is kind.
   原因：teacher 指人，使用 who。

2. 「這是我們見面的咖啡店。」常被說成 ❌ This is the café which we met.
   正確：✅ This is the café where we met.
   原因：要表達在該地點發生，使用 where。

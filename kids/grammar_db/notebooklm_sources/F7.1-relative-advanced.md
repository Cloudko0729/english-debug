## 這堂課要教什麼

使用 whose、when 與非限定關係子句，並讓補充資訊連到正確先行詞。

句型公式：**defining or non-defining relative clause with clear antecedent**

今天學完，小朋友要能自己造一句像這樣的話：
「The student whose idea won is in my class.」

## 情境對話（可以直接當開場小劇場）

A: Who checked the final design?
B: Ms. Lee, who teaches science, checked it.
A: Is she the teacher whose class won last year?
B: Yes, and June is the month when her class presents again.

請用這段對話當開場，營造真實生活情境，再帶出文法規則。

## 自然例句

1. The student whose idea won is in my class.
2. June is the month when we present our project.
3. Ms. Lee, who teaches science, checked our design.
4. The device that measures air quality is on the roof.

## 常見錯誤對照（請用「你猜猜看哪句對」的口吻帶過）

- ❌ Ms. Lee who teaches science checked our design. → ✅ Ms. Lee, who teaches science, checked our design.
  （若 Ms. Lee 身分已明確，非必要補充資訊通常以逗號隔開。）
- ❌ We spoke to the engineer about the device who designed it. → ✅ We spoke to the engineer who designed the device.
  （who 子句應緊接它所描述的 engineer，避免錯誤附著。）

## 中文母語者最容易犯的錯（請特別強調，因為是中文直翻習慣造成的）

1. 「那位腳踏車被偷的學生報警了。」常被說成 ❌ The student who bike was stolen called the police.
   正確：✅ The student whose bike was stolen called the police.
   原因：表示所屬關係使用 whose。

2. 「我們在五月完成，那是雨季開始的月份。」常被說成 ❌ We finished in May, which the rainy season begins.
   正確：✅ We finished in May, when the rainy season begins.
   原因：指時間並表示在那時發生，使用 when。

## 這堂課要教什麼

用 must、may、might、could、can't 表達有證據的不同確定程度。

句型公式：**modal + base form; modal + have + past participle for past deduction**

今天學完，小朋友要能自己造一句像這樣的話：
「The lights are on, so someone must be home.」

## 情境對話（可以直接當開場小劇場）

A: Why is the classroom door open?
B: The teacher might be inside.
A: Could a student have opened it?
B: Yes, but it can't have been Leo because he is absent.

請用這段對話當開場，營造真實生活情境，再帶出文法規則。

## 自然例句

1. The lights are on, so someone must be home.
2. This key might open the old box.
3. The answer could be in the report.
4. That can't be Ben; he is at school.

## 常見錯誤對照（請用「你猜猜看哪句對」的口吻帶過）

- ❌ Someone must to be home. → ✅ Someone must be home.
  （must 後接原形 be。）
- ❌ It must rain tomorrow because the sky is cloudy. → ✅ It might rain tomorrow because the sky is cloudy.
  （多雲只能支持可能性，使用 must 會顯得過度確定。）

## 中文母語者最容易犯的錯（請特別強調，因為是中文直翻習慣造成的）

1. 「她可能忘了。」常被說成 ❌ She must have forgotten.
   正確：✅ She might have forgotten.
   原因：只有可能性時使用 might have；must have 表強烈推論。

2. 「那不可能是真的。」常被說成 ❌ That must not be true.
   正確：✅ That can't be true.
   原因：表達邏輯上不可能時，can't be 通常更直接自然。

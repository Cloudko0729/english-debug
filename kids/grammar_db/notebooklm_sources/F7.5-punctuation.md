> 本課情境主題：簡報、文章修改與自然表達

## 這堂課要教什麼

使用冒號、分號、破折號、括號與連字號組織資訊，而不製造逗號拼接。

句型公式：**colon for introduction; semicolon for related clauses; dash/brackets for parenthesis; hyphen in compounds**

今天學完，小朋友要能自己造一句像這樣的話：
「We need three things: paper, tape, and scissors.」

## 情境對話（可以直接當開場小劇場）

A: Why can't we join these sentences with only a comma?
B: They are both complete clauses.
A: What can we use instead?
B: We can use a semicolon or add a coordinating conjunction.

請用這段對話當開場，營造真實生活情境，再帶出文法規則。

## 自然例句

1. We need three things: paper, tape, and scissors.
2. The first plan is cheaper; the second is safer.
3. The final test—our most difficult task—starts tomorrow.
4. We designed a low-cost water filter.

## 常見錯誤對照（請用「你猜猜看哪句對」的口吻帶過）

- ❌ The first plan is cheaper, the second is safer. → ✅ The first plan is cheaper; the second is safer.
  （兩個完整句不能只用逗號連接。）
- ❌ We need: paper, tape, and scissors. → ✅ We need three things: paper, tape, and scissors.
  （冒號前通常需要能獨立成立並完整引出後文的結構。）

## 中文母語者最容易犯的錯（請特別強調，因為是中文直翻習慣造成的）

1. 「報告有兩部分：問題和建議。」常被說成 ❌ The report has two parts; the problem and the recommendation.
   正確：✅ The report has two parts: the problem and the recommendation.
   原因：後面是前句引出的清單，使用冒號。

2. 「這是一個為期三天的活動。」常被說成 ❌ This is a three day event.
   正確：✅ This is a three-day event.
   原因：數字與單數名詞共同放在名詞前作複合修飾語時加連字號。

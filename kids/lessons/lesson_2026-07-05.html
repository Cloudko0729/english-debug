STEP ②

文法 Grammar：have / has + 過去分詞

現在完成式講「過去發生、但跟現在有關」的事。結構：主詞 + have / has + 過去分詞(p.p.)。

主詞配 have / has

主詞助動詞例
I / You / We / TheyhaveI have finished my homework.
He / She / IthasShe has gone to school.
I have finished my homework. 我已經寫完功課了。
She has gone to school. 她已經去學校了。

過去分詞 p.p.（規則 = +ed；不規則要背）

原形過去式過去分詞 p.p.
finishfinishedfinished
gowentgone
eatateeaten
seesawseen
bewas/werebeen
dodiddone

三大用法

① 已完成（常配 already / just / yet）
I have already done it. 我已經做好了。
② 經驗「曾經」（常配 ever / never）
Have you ever been to Japan? 你（曾經）去過日本嗎？
I have never eaten sushi. 我從沒吃過壽司。
③ 持續到現在（常配 for / since）
I have lived here for five years. 我在這裡住五年了。

否定 / 疑問

I haven't finished yet. 我還沒寫完。
Have you eaten lunch? 你吃午餐了嗎？ 短答：Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.

⚠️ 最常犯的錯

✗ I have went → ✓ I have gone（用過去分詞 gone，不是過去式 went）
✗ She have finished → ✓ She has finished
✗ Did you ever been there? → ✓ Have you ever been there?

## Reading Passage

I like animals. I have a dog and a cat at home. My dog is brown. My cat is white. At the zoo, I see a monkey, a lion, and an elephant. The elephant is very big. My favorite animal is the rabbit.

## Comprehension Questions

1. What animals does the writer have at home? → A dog and a cat.
2. What color is the cat? → White.
3. What is the writer's favorite animal? → A rabbit.

## Cloze Practice

1. The ______ is white. → rabbit / cat
2. A ______ is fast. → tiger / horse
3. The ______ is very big. → elephant

## Reading Passage

I have a body. I have a head with two eyes, two ears, one nose, and one mouth. My hair is black. I wash my face and hands every day. I have two arms and two legs. I take care of my body.

## Comprehension Questions

1. How many eyes does the writer have? → Two.
2. What color is the writer's hair? → Black.
3. What does the writer wash every day? → Face and hands.

## Cloze Practice

1. I have two ______. → eyes / ears / legs
2. I wash my ______ every day. → face / hands
3. My ______ is black. → hair

## Reading Passage

Every day, I wear my school uniform. My uniform is white and blue. I also wear white socks and black shoes. In winter, I wear a coat because it is cold. In summer, I wear shorts and a hat.

## Comprehension Questions

1. What color is the uniform? → White and blue.
2. What does the writer wear in winter? → A coat.
3. What does the writer wear in summer? → Shorts and a hat.

## Cloze Practice

1. My ______ is white and blue. → uniform
2. I wear a ______ because it is cold. → coat / jacket
3. I wear white ______. → socks

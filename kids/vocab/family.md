## Reading Passage

My name is Kevin. I live with my father, mother, and sister. My father is tall. My mother is kind. My sister likes cats. My grandma cooks well. We are a happy family.

## Comprehension Questions

1. Who lives with Kevin? → His father, mother, sister, and grandma.
2. Is Kevin's father tall? → Yes, he is.
3. What does Kevin's sister like? → She likes cats.

## Cloze Practice

1. My ______ is tall. → father
2. I have one ______. → brother / sister
3. My ______ cooks well. → grandma

## Listening Sentences

1. My family is happy.
2. My mother is kind.
3. I have one sister.

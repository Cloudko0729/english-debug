## Reading Passage

I eat rice for lunch and dinner. In the morning, I eat bread and an egg. I drink milk every morning. I like apple juice, too. On my birthday, I eat cake. My favorite food is noodles.

## Comprehension Questions

1. What does the writer eat in the morning? → Bread and an egg.
2. What does the writer drink every morning? → Milk.
3. What is the writer's favorite food? → Noodles.

## Cloze Practice

1. I eat ______ every day. → rice
2. I drink ______ every morning. → milk
3. I eat ______ on my birthday. → cake

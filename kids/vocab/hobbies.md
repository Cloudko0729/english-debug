## Reading Passage

My name is Albert. I have many hobbies. I like to read books and draw animals. After school, I play basketball with my friends. On weekends, I swim and run. My sister likes to sing and dance. My mom likes to cook. We all have fun!

## Comprehension Questions

1. What does Albert like to do? → Read books and draw animals.
2. What does Albert play after school? → Basketball.
3. What does Albert's sister like? → To sing and dance.

## Cloze Practice

1. I like to ______ books. → read
2. I play ______ after school. → basketball
3. My sister likes to ______ and dance. → sing

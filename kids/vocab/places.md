## Reading Passage

On Saturday, my family goes out. We go to the market in the morning. My mom buys food. Then we eat at a restaurant. In the afternoon, we visit the zoo. I see elephants and monkeys! We come home in the evening.

## Comprehension Questions

1. Where does the family go in the morning? → The market.
2. What do they do at the restaurant? → Eat.
3. What does the writer see at the zoo? → Elephants and monkeys.

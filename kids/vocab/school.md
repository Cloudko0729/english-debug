## Reading Passage

I go to school every day. My classroom is big and clean. My teacher is kind. I sit at my desk with my book, pencil, and eraser. I write in my notebook. My bag is a little heavy today.

## Comprehension Questions

1. Is the classroom big? → Yes, it is big and clean.
2. What does the writer write with? → A pencil.
3. Is the bag heavy? → Yes, a little.

## Cloze Practice

1. My ______ is kind. → teacher
2. I write with a ______. → pencil
3. My ______ is heavy. → bag

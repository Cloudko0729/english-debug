## Reading Passage

I go to school from Monday to Friday. In the morning, I wake up early. In the afternoon, I have lunch. In the evening, I do my homework. At night, I sleep. On Saturday and Sunday, it is the weekend. Sunday is my favorite day!

## Comprehension Questions

1. When does the writer go to school? → Monday to Friday.
2. What does the writer do in the evening? → Homework.
3. What is the writer's favorite day? → Sunday.

## Cloze Practice

1. I go to school in the ______. → morning
2. I sleep at ______. → night
3. ______ is my favorite day. → Sunday

## Reading Passage

Today is rainy. The sky is cloudy and it is cold. I take my umbrella. In summer, the weather is hot and sunny. I wear shorts and a hat. In winter, it is cold and sometimes windy. I love snow!

## Comprehension Questions

1. What is the weather like today? → Rainy and cloudy.
2. What does the writer take when it rains? → An umbrella.
3. What does the writer love in winter? → Snow.

## Cloze Practice

1. It is ______ today. I need an umbrella. → rainy / cloudy
2. Summer is hot and ______. → sunny
3. Take your ______. → umbrella

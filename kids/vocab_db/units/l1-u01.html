我是： 🪙 —

① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

a article
一個
常這樣用：a + singular noun
I have a red pen.
There is a cat by the door.
am be
是（用於 I）
常這樣用：I am
I am a student.
I am happy today.
an article
一個
常這樣用：an + vowel sound
I eat an apple after school.
She has an old book.
are be
是（用於 you／we／they）
常這樣用：you/we/they are
You are my friend.
They are in the classroom.
at preposition
在；於
常這樣用：at seven
We meet at the bus stop.
I get up at seven.
do auxiliary
做；助動詞
常這樣用：do homework
I do my homework after dinner.
Do you like this song?
he pronoun
他
常這樣用：He is my brother.
He is my brother.
He plays with the dog.
how adverb
如何；怎麼樣
常這樣用：how
How are you today?
How do you go to school?
I pronoun
我
常這樣用：I am ten years old.
I am ten years old.
I like to read.
in preposition
在……裡面
常這樣用：in the box
The ball is in the box.
We play in the park.
is be
是（用於 he／she／it）
常這樣用：he/she/it is
She is my teacher.
The milk is cold.
it pronoun
它；這件事
常這樣用：It is a sunny day.
It is a sunny day.
I found a key, and it is small.
not adverb
不
常這樣用：not
This book is not new.
I do not like cold soup.
or conjunction
或者
常這樣用：Do you want tea or water?
Do you want tea or water?
We can walk or take the bus.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A Are you at the school door?
B Yes, I am here with Ben.
A Is he in your class?
B Yes. He has a book and an apple.
A Do you have a pen?
B No, I do not. It is at home.
A Do you want a red pen or a blue pen?
B A blue pen, please.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

It is seven. I am in the kitchen with Dad. He has an egg and a red cup. The cup is on the table. We are ready for breakfast. I do not want tea. I want milk. Is it hot or cold? Dad says it is cold. I eat the egg at home.

I am at the park with my sister. She is on a red bike, and I am in a little car. It is a toy car, but it is big for me. We do not go fast. Are we near the tree or the bus? We are near the tree.

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

a / an：看聲音，不只看字母

兩者都放在單數可數名詞前。下一個字以母音聲音開始用 an，其他用 a。

I have a red bag.
She eats an apple.
He waits for an hour.
This is a useful book.
am / is / are：主詞決定 be 動詞

I 配 am；he、she、it 和單數配 is；you、we、they 和複數配 are。情態動詞或 to 後使用 be。

I am ready.
The dog is under the desk.
They are good friends.
Please be quiet.
do / does：一般動詞問句

I、you、we、they 用 do；he、she、it 用 does。用了 does，後面的動詞回原形。

Do you like milk?
Does Amy walk to school?
We do our homework.
He does his homework.
I / you / he / she / it / we / they

代名詞替代人物或物品。I 永遠大寫；it 通常代替單一物品或動物；we 包含說話者。

I am a student.
She is my teacher.
It is a new book.
They are in the library.
in / on / at：位置與時間

in 表示在裡面或較大時間；on 表示接觸表面或日期；at 表示一個點或精確時間。

The pencil is in the box.
The book is on the desk.
We meet at school.
Class starts at eight.
and / but / or / so

and 加上相同方向資訊；but 表示轉折；or 提供選擇；so 表示前因後果。

I have a pen and a ruler.
The bag is old, but it is clean.
Do you want tea or water?
I was tired, so I went home.

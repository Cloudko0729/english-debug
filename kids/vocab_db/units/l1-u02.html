① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

say verb
說
常這樣用：say hello
Please say your name.
She says hello to the new student.
she pronoun
她
常這樣用：She is my sister.
She is my sister.
She has a new bag.
that determiner
那個
常這樣用：That is our bus.
That is our bus.
That tree is tall.
they pronoun
他們；她們；它們
常這樣用：They are good friends.
They are good friends.
They walk to school together.
too adverb
也；太
常這樣用：too
I like apples, too.
This bag is too heavy.
we pronoun
我們
常這樣用：We are in the same class.
We are in the same class.
We eat lunch at school.
what determiner
什麼
常這樣用：What is your name?
What is your name?
What do you want for lunch?
who pronoun
誰
常這樣用：Who is that boy?
Who is that boy?
Who has my ruler?
yes interjection
是；好
常這樣用：a yes
Yes, I am ready.
She said yes to the picnic.
you pronoun
你；你們
常這樣用：You are very kind.
You are very kind.
Do you want some water?
eight number
八
常這樣用：eight + plural noun
I eat eight grapes.
Eight fish are in the bowl.
five number
五
常這樣用：five + plural noun
I have five fingers.
Five students play ball.
four number
四
常這樣用：four + plural noun
Four cups are on the table.

nine number
九
常這樣用：nine + plural noun
Nine kids run fast.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A What is in that big box?
B It is a birthday cake.
A Who is the cake for?
B It is for Amy. She is nine today.
A Do we have eight cups?
B Yes, and we have four plates, too.
A Can you say, "Happy birthday" with us?
B Yes! We are ready.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

Today we have a class party. We put five red cups and four blue cups on the table. That is nine cups. What do we need now? We need cake, too. They bring the cake, and she brings water. Who has the plates? You do. Yes, we are ready.

A girl and a boy are at the school store. She wants eight pencils, and he wants five pens. They see four red bags and nine blue bags. What bag do you like? The girl says, "That red bag." The boy says, "I like that bag, too."

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

I / you / he / she / it / we / they

代名詞替代人物或物品。I 永遠大寫；it 通常代替單一物品或動物；we 包含說話者。

I am a student.
She is my teacher.
It is a new book.
They are in the library.
this / that / these / those：距離與單複數

近處單數 this、遠處單數 that；近處複數 these、遠處複數 those。

This pencil is mine.
That bus is ours.
These books are new.
Those trees are tall.
say / speak / talk

say 重點是說出的內容；speak 常接語言或正式發言；talk 表示交談，常用 talk to/with/about。

Please say your name.
I can speak English.
I talk with my friend.
We talk about the movie.

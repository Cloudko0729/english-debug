我是： 🪙 —

① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

arm noun
手臂
常這樣用：an arm
My arm is strong.
I raise my arm in class.
ear noun
耳朵
常這樣用：an ear
My ear hears music.
The ear is small.
eye noun
眼睛
常這樣用：an eye
My eye sees a bird.
One eye is open.
fat adjective
胖的
常這樣用：be fat
The cat is fat.
This pig is short and fat.
fine adjective
很好；沒事
常這樣用：be fine
I am fine, thank you.
The weather is fine today.
hot adjective
熱
常這樣用：be hot
The soup is hot.
It is hot in summer.
leg noun
腿
常這樣用：a leg
My leg kicks the ball.
A leg helps me run.
neck noun
脖子
常這樣用：a neck
This is my neck.
My neck feels fine.
man noun
男人
常這樣用：a man
The man is waiting for the bus.
That man is my teacher.
Mr. title
先生
常這樣用：a mr
Mr. Lin is our teacher.
I say hello to Mr. Wang.
Mrs. title
太太
常這樣用：a mrs
Mrs. Chen has a small store.
I gave the card to Mrs. Lee.
Ms. title
女士
常這樣用：a ms
Ms. Wu teaches English.
Please ask Ms. Lin for help.
name noun
名字
常這樣用：a name
My name is Tina.
Please write your name on the card.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A What is wrong with your arm?
B It hurts when I move it.
A Does your leg hurt, too?
B No, my leg is fine.
A Is your neck hot?
B No, but my ear is hot.
A Can you see with this eye?
B Yes. I can see you.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

Tom is at the school health room. His arm and leg hurt. His neck is fine, but one eye is red. The nurse looks at his ear, too. Tom is hot and sad. He sees a fat cat by the door. The cat makes him smile.

A man is at the school door. His name is Mr. Brown. Mrs. Green is with him. They need help, so Ms. Hill comes. She says, "My name is Anna. Come with me." The man and Mrs. Green go to the school office. Now they are fine.

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

hot / warm / cool

hot 是很熱；warm 是舒適的溫暖；cool 是稍冷或涼爽。

The soup is hot.
The tea is warm.
The evening air is cool.
Summer days are hot.
good / fine / nice / great

good 是一般的好；fine 常回答身體狀況沒問題；nice 表示令人愉快或待人友善；great 比 good 更強。

The food is good.
I am fine, thank you.
She is nice to everyone.
You did a great job.

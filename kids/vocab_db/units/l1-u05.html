① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

bird noun
鳥
常這樣用：a bird
A bird sings in the tree.
The bird has small wings.
cat noun
貓
常這樣用：a cat
The cat sleeps on a chair.
My cat likes fish.
dog noun
狗
常這樣用：a dog
My dog likes milk.
The dog runs in the park.
lion noun
獅子
常這樣用：a lion
The lion is strong.
A lion lives at the zoo.
moon noun
月亮
常這樣用：a moon
The moon is bright tonight.
We can see the moon in the night sky.
pet noun
寵物
常這樣用：a pet
My pet is a small dog.
We take our pet to the park.
pig noun
豬
常這樣用：a pig
I see a pig.
The pig is near the tree.
sky noun
天空
常這樣用：a sky
The sky is blue today.
I can see a kite in the sky.
sun noun
太陽
常這樣用：a sun
The sun is bright and warm.
Do not look straight at the sun.
tree noun
樹
常這樣用：a tree
A bird is in the tree.
We planted a small tree by the house.
nose noun
鼻子
常這樣用：a nose
My nose smells soup.
The nose is on my face.
sad noun
難過的
常這樣用：a sad
He is sad.
The sad boy sits alone.
card noun
卡片
常這樣用：a card
I made a card for Grandma.
Write your name on the card.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A Is that your dog by the tree?
B Yes. He is my new pet.
A Why is your cat up there?
B She sees a bird in the sky.
A Is the bird near the sun?
B No, it is under the tree.
A Your dog looks sad now.
B He wants the cat to come down.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

Our dog is a good pet, but he likes to run. At the park, he sees a bird and runs to a tree. A cat is under the tree. The cat sees a pig on a card. Our dog sees it, too. He wants to play with them all.

The sun is in the sky, and a bird sits in our tree. My cat looks up with her nose in the air. At night, the moon comes out. My little pet is sad because the bird is not there. I give her a lion toy, and she is happy.

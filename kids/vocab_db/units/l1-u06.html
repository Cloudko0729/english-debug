我是： 🪙 —

① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

cup noun
杯子
常這樣用：cup
Please give me a cup of water.
The blue cup is on the table.
egg noun
蛋
常這樣用：egg
The egg is white.
I cook an egg for lunch.
food noun
食物
常這樣用：food
We need food and water.
The food at this restaurant is good.
lunch noun
午餐
常這樣用：eat lunch
I eat lunch at school.
We have rice and vegetables for lunch.
milk noun
牛奶
常這樣用：milk
Milk is in my cup.
I drink milk every morning.
pie noun
派
常這樣用：pie
We made an apple pie.
The pie is on the table.
tea noun
茶
常這樣用：tea
I drink warm tea in the morning.
The tea is on the table.
water noun
水
常這樣用：water
I drink water after running.
The water in the cup is warm.
desk noun
書桌
常這樣用：a desk
My desk is tidy.
A pencil is on the desk.
pen noun
原子筆
常這樣用：a pen
I use a pen at school.
The pen is on my desk.
picture noun
圖片；照片
常這樣用：a picture
I draw a picture of my family.
The picture is on my desk.
ball noun
球
常這樣用：a ball
I throw the ball to Ben.
The red ball is under the chair.
bed noun
床
常這樣用：a bed
There is a bed in the room.
The bed is clean.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A What do you want for lunch?
B I want an egg and some rice.
A Do you want milk or tea?
B Milk, please. Is it in the cup?
A Yes, and the water is here.
B Can we have the pie, too?
A Yes, but eat your food first.
B Okay. I am ready to eat.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

It is lunch time at home. Dad puts an egg and rice on my plate. Mom gives me a cup of water. My sister has milk, and Grandma has tea. A pie is on the table, too. We have good food. We sit down and eat lunch together.

My room is ready for art time. A pen and a cup are on my desk. I draw a picture of a red ball by my bed. Then I draw milk, tea, and an egg. The picture is for Mom. I put it on her door.

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

breakfast / lunch / dinner

breakfast 是早上第一餐；lunch 是中午餐；dinner 通常是晚餐或一天的主餐。

I eat breakfast at seven.
We have lunch at school.
Dad cooks dinner at six.
Dinner is in the dining room.

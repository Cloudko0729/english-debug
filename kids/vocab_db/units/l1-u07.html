我是： 🪙 —

① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

come verb
來
常這樣用：come here
I come home early.
She comes to class.
go verb
去
常這樣用：go home
I go to school.
We go home at five.
jog verb
慢跑
常這樣用：jog + object/place
I jog every day.
We jog together.
love verb
愛
常這樣用：love + object/place
I love my family.
He loves his dog.
miss verb
想念；錯過
常這樣用：miss + object/place
I miss my friend.
Do not miss the school bus.
run verb
跑步
常這樣用：run + object/place
I run fast.
The boy runs in the park.
sit verb
坐
常這樣用：sit + object/place
I sit on a chair.
He sits by the window.
door noun
門
常這樣用：a door
There is a door in the room.
The door is clean.
room noun
房間
常這樣用：in the room
My room is small but clean.
There are two windows in the room.
toy noun
玩具
常這樣用：a toy
The baby has a new toy.
Please put the toy in the box.
bus noun
公車
常這樣用：a bus
The bus is near my home.
We can see the bus from here.
car noun
汽車
常這樣用：a car
The car is near my home.
We can see the car from here.
home noun
家
常這樣用：go home
I go home after school.
My mother is at home.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A Do you go home by bus?
B No, I go home in Dad's car.
A Can you come to the park first?
B Yes. I want to run with you.
A Do you jog every day?
B No, I sit and play with my toy.
A I miss our park games.
B Me too. Let us go now.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

I come home at four. My dog and I go to the park. I jog, and he can run. We love this game. Then I sit by the door and watch him. I miss Mom, but she comes home soon. We all go into the room together.

A toy car is in my room. I sit on the bed and make it go. The car runs to the door. Now it is under a toy bus. Dad comes home and looks in. "Come with me," he says. We go to the real car and ride to Grandma's home.

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

come / go：移動方向

come 是朝說話者或目的地靠近；go 是離開目前位置前往別處。

Please come here.
Dad comes home at six.
I go to school by bus.
We go there on Sunday.
home / house / room

house 是建築物；room 是房子裡的一個房間；home 強調居住與歸屬，可直接說 go home。

My house has three rooms.
My room is small.
Mom is at home.
I go home after school.

① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

bad adjective
壞的
常這樣用：be bad
The milk is bad.
That is a bad smell.
big adjective
大的
常這樣用：be big
The elephant is big.
I have a big school bag.
good adjective
好的
常這樣用：be good
This is a good book.
The soup smells good.
great adjective
很棒的
常這樣用：be great
You did a great job.
We had a great day at the zoo.
new adjective
新的
常這樣用：be new
I have a new bike.
This is a new book.
nice adjective
好的
常這樣用：be nice
She is nice to everyone.
It is a nice day for a picnic.
red adjective
紅色
常這樣用：be red
The apple is red.
I wear a red shirt.
tall adjective
高的
常這樣用：be tall
My brother is tall.
That is a tall tree.
here adverb
這裡
常這樣用：here
My bag is here.
Please come here and sit down.
job noun
工作；職業
常這樣用：have a job
My father has a new job.
Teaching is an important job.
no interjection
不；沒有
常這樣用：a no
No, that is not my pen.
There is no milk in the cup.
OK interjection
好；可以
常這樣用：an ok
OK, I can help you.
Is it OK to sit here?
walk verb
走路
常這樣用：walk + object/place
I walk to school.
She walks with Mom.

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

here / there：說話者的位置

here 是靠近說話者；there 是離說話者較遠的位置。

My bag is here.
The bus stop is over there.
Come here, please.
There is a bird in that tree.
job / work / worker

job 是一份職業或工作；work 可指工作這件事或動詞；worker 是做工作的人。

My father has a new job.
My parents work in the city.
We have a lot of work today.
The worker fixes the road.
big / small / tall / long / short

big/small 說整體大小；tall 說由下到上的高度；long/short 說長度，short 也可說身高較矮。

The elephant is big.
The ant is small.
The tree is tall.
The road is long.
good / fine / nice / great

good 是一般的好；fine 常回答身體狀況沒問題；nice 表示令人愉快或待人友善；great 比 good 更強。

The food is good.
I am fine, thank you.
She is nice to everyone.
You did a great job.

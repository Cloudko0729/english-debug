② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

📖 有虛線的字是還沒學過的，點一下會出現中文。

A Please put these cups on the table.
B Okay好、可以. What about those plates盤子?
A Put them under the birthday sign標記.
B Is it a real cake in that box?
A Yes, and its color is pink.
B Wow! The party聚會 starts at three, right正確的?
A Yes. Look at the clock.
B It is three now. Let us start!

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

this / that / these / those：距離與單複數

近處單數 this、遠處單數 that；近處複數 these、遠處複數 those。

This pencil is mine.
That bus is ours.
These books are new.
Those trees are tall.
in / on / at：位置與時間

in 表示在裡面或較大時間；on 表示接觸表面或日期；at 表示一個點或精確時間。

The pencil is in the box.
The book is on the desk.
We meet at school.
Class starts at eight.
under / in front of / next to

under 是正下方；in front of 是前方；next to 是緊鄰旁邊。

The cat is under the chair.
The bus is in front of the school.
The bank is next to the bookstore.
My shoes are under the bed.
and / but / or / so

and 加上相同方向資訊；but 表示轉折；or 提供選擇；so 表示前因後果。

I have a pen and a ruler.
The bag is old, but it is clean.
Do you want tea or water?
I was tired, so I went home.

① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

brother noun
兄弟
常這樣用：a brother
My brother plays soccer.
Brother shares his juice.
classmate noun
同學
常這樣用：a classmate
Ben is my new classmate.
I sit next to my classmate.
father noun
爸爸
常這樣用：a father
My father reads a book.
Father cooks dinner today.
singer noun
歌手
常這樣用：a singer
The singer has a beautiful voice.
My aunt is a singer.
sister noun

常這樣用：a sister

Sister sings with me.
son noun

Their son is six years old.
Mr. Lee walks to school with his son.
student noun

A student sits at a desk.

woman noun

air noun

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A Is that woman your teacher?
B Yes. She is our music teacher.
A Is the singer your sister?
B No, she is my classmate's sister.
A Where is your brother?
B He is next to my father and son.
A Can every student join the song?
B Yes. Our class sings together.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

Our school has a family music show. My sister is a singer, and my brother plays the drum. My father sits with my little son. A woman helps each student get ready. My classmate stands by our teacher. The air in the hall is cool. After the song, every family smiles and claps.

A teacher takes her class outside for nature week. One student sees an ant on a flower. Another child finds a bee in the air. My classmate shows me his toe because an ant is near it. A woman helps him stand up. We laugh and talk about the small animals we see this year.

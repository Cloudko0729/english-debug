① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

📖 有虛線的字是還沒學過的，點一下會出現中文。

A What animal is near the flower?
B It is a small frog.
A Can you see the fox, too?
B Yes. It is by the sheep.
A Where is the hippo?
B It is in the water.
A Do you want to draw this zoo?
B Yes. My book is in the classroom.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

📖 有虛線的字是還沒學過的，點一下會出現中文。

Our class班級 visits a small zoo. A cow and a sheep eat near the tree. A hen walks by the flower, and a frog sits under it. We see a fox, a hippo, and many birds. At lunch, we eat fruit by the sea animal room. Then we draw our favorite最喜愛的 animal in a book.

Today our classroom becomes成為 an animal art美術 room. Each student takes a book from the box and sits on a chair. I draw a fox near a flower. My friend draws a frog by the sea. The teacher adds a cow, a hen, a hippo, and a sheep to one big zoo picture. We put it on the classroom wall.

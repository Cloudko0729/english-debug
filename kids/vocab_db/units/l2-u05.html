① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

close verb
關上
常這樣用：close + object/place
I close the book.
She closes the window.
give verb
給
常這樣用：give + object/place
I give Mom a flower.
He gives me a pencil.
play verb
玩
常這樣用：play + object/place
I play with my friend.
We play basketball.
see verb
看見
常這樣用：see a bird
I see a cat.
She sees a rainbow.
sing verb
唱歌
常這樣用：sing + object/place
I sing a song.
She sings in class.
rice noun
米飯
常這樣用：rice
I eat rice for lunch.
The rice is in my bowl.
sandwich noun
三明治
常這樣用：sandwich
I made a sandwich for lunch.
The sandwich has ham and egg in it.
soup noun
湯
常這樣用：soup
Soup is hot.
I eat soup for dinner.
cap noun
帽子
常這樣用：a cap
I have a cap.
The cap is clean.
shirt noun
襯衫
常這樣用：a shirt
His shirt is white.
I fold my shirt neatly.
short adjective
短的
常這樣用：be short
This pencil is short.
The boy has short hair.
doll noun
洋娃娃
常這樣用：a doll
There is a doll in the room.
The doll is clean.
sofa noun
沙發
常這樣用：a sofa
There is a sofa in the room.
The sofa is clean.
ship noun
船
常這樣用：a ship
The ship is near my home.
We can see the ship from here.
store noun
商店
常這樣用：a store
The store sells shoes.
I buy milk at the store.

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

big / small / tall / long / short

big/small 說整體大小；tall 說由下到上的高度；long/short 說長度，short 也可說身高較矮。

The elephant is big.
The ant is small.
The tree is tall.
The road is long.

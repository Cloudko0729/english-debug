① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

but conjunction
但是
常這樣用：The bag is old, but it is clean.
The bag is old, but it is clean.
I am tired, but I can help.
color noun
顏色
常這樣用：a color
Red is my favorite color.
What color is your bag?
hello interjection
哈囉
常這樣用：a hello
Hello, my name is Amy.
I say hello to my teacher.
hey interjection
嘿、喂
常這樣用：a hey
Hey, wait for me!
Hey, that is my ball.
hi interjection
嗨
常這樣用：a hi
Hi, Ben! How are you?
She says hi to her classmate.
oh interjection
噢、啊、唉
常這樣用：an oh
Oh, I see your bag.
Oh, this cake is good.
thank interjection
感謝
常這樣用：a thank
I thank Dad for his help.
Thank you for the nice card.
wow interjection
哎喲、哇
常這樣用：a wow
Wow! That kite is big.
Wow! You can swim fast.
shy adjective
害羞的
常這樣用：be shy
The new student is shy.
My little sister feels shy around new people.
slow adjective
慢的
常這樣用：be slow
The turtle is slow.
This bus is slow.
small adjective
小的
常這樣用：be small
The ant is small.
She lives in a small house.
white adjective
白色
常這樣用：be white
The milk is white.
A white bird flies.
yellow adjective
黃色
常這樣用：be yellow
The banana is yellow.
I see a yellow bus.
picnic noun
野餐
常這樣用：a picnic
We have a picnic in the park.
Please bring fruit to the picnic.
song noun
歌曲
常這樣用：a song
We sing a song in music class.
This song is easy to learn.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A Hi! Can I sit here for the picnic?
B Yes, but this mat is very small.
A Oh, I can sit by the yellow bag.
B Hey, that is my white cap!
A Wow, it looks nice on you.
B Thank you. I am a little shy.
A Do you want to sing our song?
B Yes, but please sing slowly.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

We have a picnic by the school. I say hello to a shy girl in a white cap. She says hi, but she talks very slowly. We show her our small yellow ball and green cups. "Wow, I like the colors," she says. We thank her and ask her to sing a song. Soon we are all friends.

A small boy comes to the music room. "Hey, what is your favorite color?" I ask. He says yellow, but his guitar is white. "Oh, it is very nice!" I say. He is shy and plays a slow song. At the end, everyone says, "Wow!" The boy smiles and says, "Thank you."

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

and / but / or / so

and 加上相同方向資訊；but 表示轉折；or 提供選擇；so 表示前因後果。

I have a pen and a ruler.
The bag is old, but it is clean.
Do you want tea or water?
I was tired, so I went home.
big / small / tall / long / short

big/small 說整體大小；tall 說由下到上的高度；long/short 說長度，short 也可說身高較矮。

The elephant is big.
The ant is small.
The tree is tall.
The road is long.

① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

a.m. adverb
上午
常這樣用：a.m.
School starts at eight a.m.
I get up at seven a.m.
after preposition
在……之後
常這樣用：I wash my hands after lunch.
I wash my hands after lunch.
We play outside after school.
afternoon noun
下午
常這樣用：in the afternoon
Afternoon is time for class.
We play in the afternoon.
ago adverb
……以前
常這樣用：ago
We met two days ago.
She called me an hour ago.
eleven number
十一
常這樣用：eleven + plural noun
Eleven apples are in the bag.
I see eleven balloons.
evening noun
傍晚
常這樣用：in the evening
Evening is time for dinner.
I walk home in the evening.
every determiner
每一個
常這樣用：I read every day.
I read every day.
Every student has a book.
hour noun
小時
常這樣用：a hour
One hour is long.
I study for one hour.
about preposition
關於；大約
常這樣用：This book is about animals.
This book is about animals.
We talk about our weekend.
true adjective
真的；正確的
常這樣用：be true
The story is true.
Is it true that pandas can swim?
when adverb
何時
常這樣用：when
When is your birthday?
Tell me when you are ready.
where adverb
哪裡
常這樣用：where
Where is my book?
Where do you live?
why adverb
為什麼
常這樣用：why
Why are you sad?
Why do you like this story?
with preposition
和……一起；用
常這樣用：I go to school with my sister.
I go to school with my sister.
Write your name with a pen.
word noun
單字
常這樣用：a word
Read the word on the card.
I use this word in a sentence.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

📖 有虛線的字是還沒學過的，點一下會出現中文。

A When can we study our English words together?
B How about eleven a.m. after breakfast?
A I have school then. Is the afternoon OK?
B Yes. Where do you want to study?
A At my house, with my sister.
B Great. We can study for one hour.
A Why do you need these word cards?
B Because I read them every evening.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

📖 有虛線的字是還沒學過的，點一下會出現中文。

At eleven a.m., our English teacher gives us a new word. We read it together and ask what it is about. Ben knows where it is in the book, but he does not know why it is there. After an hour, we can say the word and write it. Every child feels great because what we say is true. We will study together every afternoon.

Two hours ago, Amy called me after school. She wanted help with three English words, so she walked to my home. We read the words with my sister in the afternoon. When the sun was down, Amy asked where she could put her book. I put her book in a bag. In the evening, she walked home and said說（say 的過去式）, "I know every word now." I said說（say 的過去式）, "Great!" because it was true.

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

morning / afternoon / evening

morning 是起床到中午前；afternoon 是中午後；evening 是傍晚到睡前。

I eat breakfast in the morning.
We have art in the afternoon.
I eat dinner in the evening.
The sky gets dark in the evening.
today / yesterday / tomorrow / ago

today 是今天；yesterday 是昨天；tomorrow 是明天；ago 從現在往前數一段時間。

Today is sunny.
Yesterday was rainy.
Tomorrow will be warm.
We met two days ago.

① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

aunt noun
阿姨
常這樣用：an aunt
My aunt makes cake.
Aunt gives me a book.
child noun
孩子
常這樣用：a child
The child is reading a book.
Every child needs a pencil.
children noun
一個以上的孩童、child 的複數
常這樣用：a children
The children are playing outside.
These children are in my class.
dad noun
爸爸
常這樣用：a dad
Dad reads the news after dinner.
I help Dad wash the car.
daddy noun
爸爸
常這樣用：a daddy
Daddy, please read this story.
My daddy makes breakfast on Sunday.
family noun
家庭
常這樣用：a family
My family eats dinner together.
Our family goes to the park.
friend noun
朋友
常這樣用：a friend
Amy is my best friend.
I talk with my friend after class.
uncle noun
叔叔
常這樣用：an uncle
My uncle drives a car.
Uncle visits us on Sunday.
worker noun
工作者
常這樣用：a factory worker
The worker fixes the road.
A worker wears a yellow hat.
morning noun
早上
常這樣用：in the morning
Morning is time for breakfast.
I read in the morning.
o'clock adverb
……點鐘
常這樣用：o'clock
It is nine o'clock.
Lunch starts at twelve o'clock.
p.m. adverb
下午；晚上
常這樣用：p.m.
The movie starts at three p.m.
I do my homework at six p.m.
start verb
開始
常這樣用：start + object/place
Class starts at eight.
We start our homework after dinner.
yesterday adverb
昨天
常這樣用：yesterday was
Yesterday was rainy.
I saw Tom yesterday.
zero number
零(num) 零點、零度
常這樣用：zero + plural noun
I have zero apples.
Zero birds are in the tree.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A Dad, what time does the family picnic start?
B It starts at ten o'clock tomorrow morning.
A Are Aunt May and Uncle Sam coming?
B Yes, and they are bringing the children.
A Can I invite my friend Leo too?
B Of course. His family can join us.
A Great! I will call him before eight p.m.
B Good. Please help Daddy pack the food first.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

Yesterday morning, my aunt and uncle came to our house at nine o'clock. My uncle is a factory worker, and he usually starts work very early. He had a free day, so our whole family ate breakfast together. Dad made eggs while my aunt cut fruit for the children. My little cousin is still a young child, but she helped carry the cups. After breakfast, Daddy took a family photo, and everyone smiled. They stayed until after lunch.

Our school sports day started at two p.m. yesterday. My friend Ben came with his family, including his aunt, uncle, and two children. His dad helped at the starting line, and my daddy gave water to the runners. At three o'clock, the relay race began. Our team had zero points at first, but we did not give up. We ran carefully and finished second. Every child cheered, and the adults clapped for us. We talked about it all afternoon.

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

job / work / worker

job 是一份職業或工作；work 可指工作這件事或動詞；worker 是做工作的人。

My father has a new job.
My parents work in the city.
We have a lot of work today.
The worker fixes the road.
child / children / kid

child 是正式的單數；children 是不規則複數；kid 是較口語的孩子。

The child is five years old.
The children play outside.
That kid is my friend.
Every child needs help sometimes.
morning / afternoon / evening

morning 是起床到中午前；afternoon 是中午後；evening 是傍晚到睡前。

I eat breakfast in the morning.
We have art in the afternoon.
I eat dinner in the evening.
The sky gets dark in the evening.
today / yesterday / tomorrow / ago

today 是今天；yesterday 是昨天；tomorrow 是明天；ago 從現在往前數一段時間。

Today is sunny.
Yesterday was rainy.
Tomorrow will be warm.
We met two days ago.

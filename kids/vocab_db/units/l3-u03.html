① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

animal noun
動物
常這樣用：an animal
I see an animal.
The animal is near the tree.
duck noun
鴨子
常這樣用：a duck
I see a duck.
The duck is near the tree.
elephant noun
大象
常這樣用：an elephant
The elephant is very big.
An elephant has a long nose.
horse noun
馬
常這樣用：a horse
The horse runs on the farm.
A horse eats grass.
kite noun
風箏
常這樣用：a kite
We fly a kite in the park.
The red kite is high in the sky.
star noun
星星
常這樣用：a star
We can see a bright star tonight.
Draw a star next to your name.
weather noun
天氣
常這樣用：check the weather
The weather is sunny today.
We check the weather before our trip.
body noun
身體
常這樣用：a body
My body needs water.
The body has arms and legs.
feel verb
感覺
常這樣用：feel + object/place
I feel happy today.
The water feels warm.
hair noun
頭髮
常這樣用：a hair
My hair is black.
I wash my hair every night.
hand noun
手
常這樣用：a hand
My hand holds a pencil.
I wash my hand before lunch.
head noun
頭
常這樣用：a head
My head hurts today.
A hat covers my head.
hungry noun
餓的
常這樣用：a hungry
I am hungry.
The hungry dog eats rice.
bag noun
書包
常這樣用：a bag
My bag is heavy.
I put books in my bag.
chalk noun
粉筆
常這樣用：a chalk
The teacher writes with chalk.
There is a piece of chalk by the board.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A Look at that duck near the pond!
B It is hiding its head under its wing.
A Do you think the animal is hungry?
B Maybe, but the sign says we should not feed it.
A The weather is getting windy now.
B Then let's put our chalk and pictures in the bag.
A Can we fly our kite before we leave?
B Sure, but hold the string tightly in your hand.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

Our class visited a small farm when the weather was cool. I fed a brown horse with my hand and watched a duck swim across the pond. The horse moved its head close to my bag because it could smell my lunch. I felt nervous at first, but its soft hair tickled my arm. Soon my whole body relaxed. By noon, I was hungry too, so I ate under a tree and drew each animal with chalk.

A strong wind blew through the park, so Nina and I took out our kite. She held it above her head while I ran across the grass. The kite climbed high, but the string rubbed my hand and made it sore. We stopped because I did not feel well. After a short rest and a drink, I felt better. The evening weather became calm, and the first star appeared. We packed the kite into its bag and walked home.

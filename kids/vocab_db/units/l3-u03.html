① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

animal noun
動物
常這樣用：an animal
I see an animal.
The animal is near the tree.
duck noun
鴨子
常這樣用：a duck
I see a duck.
The duck is near the tree.
elephant noun
大象
常這樣用：an elephant
The elephant is very big.
An elephant has a long nose.
horse noun
馬
常這樣用：a horse
The horse runs on the farm.
A horse eats grass.
kite noun
風箏
常這樣用：a kite
We fly a kite in the park.
The red kite is high in the sky.
star noun
星星
常這樣用：a star
We can see a bright star tonight.
Draw a star next to your name.
weather noun
天氣
常這樣用：check the weather
The weather is sunny today.
We check the weather before our trip.
body noun
身體
常這樣用：a body
My body needs water.
The body has arms and legs.
feel verb
感覺
常這樣用：feel + object/place
I feel happy today.
The water feels warm.
hair noun
頭髮
常這樣用：a hair
My hair is black.
I wash my hair every night.
hand noun
手
常這樣用：a hand
My hand holds a pencil.
I wash my hand before lunch.
head noun
頭
常這樣用：a head
My head hurts today.
A hat covers my head.
hungry noun
餓的
常這樣用：a hungry
I am hungry.
The hungry dog eats rice.
bag noun
書包
常這樣用：a bag
My bag is heavy.
I put books in my bag.
chalk noun
粉筆
常這樣用：a chalk
The teacher writes with chalk.
There is a piece of chalk by the board.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

📖 有虛線的字是還沒學過的，點一下會出現中文。

A Why is that duck under the tree?
B It may feel hot in this weather.
A Can we give the animal some water?
B Yes, put the cup by its head.
A My hand is dirty from the chalk.
B Your bag has water. Please wash it.
A After that, can we fly my kite?
B Yes, we can fly it this evening.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

📖 有虛線的字是還沒學過的，點一下會出現中文。

The weather was hot when our teacher took拿（take 的過去式） us to the zoo. I saw看見（see 的過去式） a big elephant take food with its nose. A child asked why the animal had no hair on its body. A duck put its head down and sat坐（sit 的過去式） under a tree. I put my hand in my bag and found找到（find 的過去式） an apple. I was hungry, so I ate吃（eat 的過去式） it with my lunch.

Ben and I went去（go 的過去式） to the sea with a kite. The weather was fine, so Ben started to run. The kite went去（go 的過去式） up, but it came來（come 的過去式） down into a tree. Ben was sad and put his head on my arm. I asked him to help me. He took拿（take 的過去式） my hand, and we got得到（get 的過去式） it down together. The kite was fine. A star was in the evening sky when we walked home.

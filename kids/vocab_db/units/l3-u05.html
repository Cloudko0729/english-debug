① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

hot dog noun
熱狗
常這樣用：hot dog
I have a hot dog for lunch.
The hot dog is on the plate.
ice noun
冰
常這樣用：ice
Please put some ice in my water.
The ice melts in the warm sun.
ice cream noun
冰淇淋
常這樣用：ice cream
I like ice cream.
Ice cream is on the table.
juice noun
果汁
常這樣用：juice
Juice is sweet.
I drink juice with lunch.
salt noun
鹽
常這樣用：salt
Please put some salt in the soup.
Too much salt is not healthy.
dining room noun
飯廳
常這樣用：a dining room
We eat dinner in the dining room.
The dining room has a large table.
house noun
房子
常這樣用：a small house
My house has three rooms.
There is a tree in front of the house.
kitchen noun
廚房
常這樣用：a kitchen
Mom is cooking in the kitchen.
The cups are in the kitchen.
phone noun
電話
常這樣用：a phone
The phone is ringing.
I talk to Grandma on the phone.
telephone noun
電話
常這樣用：a telephone
The telephone is next to the sofa.
I use the telephone to call Grandpa.
coat noun
外套
常這樣用：a coat
The coat is brown.
I wear a coat in winter.
dress noun
洋裝
常這樣用：wear a dress
The dress is pretty.
My sister wears a dress.
hat noun
帽子
常這樣用：a hat
The hat is on my head.
I wear a hat in the sun.
wear verb
穿著
常這樣用：wear a coat
I wear a coat in winter.
She wears a white shirt.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A What should we take to the picnic?
B We have hot dogs and juice in the kitchen.
A Can we bring ice cream too?
B It may melt before we reach the park.
A Then I will put some ice in this box.
B Good idea. Please wear your coat and hat.
A Should I call Amy on the phone?
B Yes. Tell her to meet us outside the house.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

It was hot after school, so my sister and I made ice cream in our kitchen. She mixed milk with ice while I poured juice into small cups. I added a little salt to the ice, but none went into the food. Mom set the dining room table and called our cousins. When they reached our house, we put away their coats and hats. Everyone enjoyed the cold dessert, and we saved one cup for Dad.

Rain started while Lily was walking home in her new dress. She quickly put on her coat and hat, but her shoes still got wet. At the house, the telephone was ringing in the dining room. Lily answered it, and Dad asked her to warm dinner in the kitchen. She made a hot dog, poured some juice, and added a little salt to her soup. Later, she called Dad on her phone and said everything was ready.

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

home / house / room

house 是建築物；room 是房子裡的一個房間；home 強調居住與歸屬，可直接說 go home。

My house has three rooms.
My room is small.
Mom is at home.
I go home after school.
phone / telephone / cellphone

phone 是最常用總稱；telephone 可指電話或較正式說法；cellphone 特別指行動電話。

I call Mom on the phone.
What is your telephone number?
My cellphone is in my bag.
The office telephone is on the desk.

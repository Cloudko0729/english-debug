① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

America noun
美國
常這樣用：an america
My aunt lives in America.
America is a large country.
bicycle noun
腳踏車
常這樣用：a bicycle
The bicycle is near my home.
We can see the bicycle from here.
bike noun
腳踏車
常這樣用：a bike
The bike is near my home.
We can see the bike from here.
drive verb
開車
常這樣用：drive + object/place
My father drives to work.
Please drive slowly on this road.
into preposition
進入……裡面
常這樣用：Put the books into the box.
Put the books into the box.
The dog runs into the room.
train noun
火車
常這樣用：a train
We take the train to the city.
The train stops at this station.
trip noun
旅行
常這樣用：a trip
Our class trip is on Friday.
I packed a hat for the trip.
truck noun
卡車
常這樣用：a truck
The truck is near my home.
We can see the truck from here.
ask noun
問
常這樣用：an ask
I ask a question.
He asks for help.
call verb
打電話
常這樣用：call + object/place
I call my mom.
She calls her friend.
cry verb
哭
常這樣用：cry + object/place
The baby cries when she is hungry.
Do not cry; I can help you.
dance verb
跳舞
常這樣用：dance + object/place
I dance with my friends.
We dance in music class.
die verb
死亡
常這樣用：die + object/place
Plants die without water.
The old tree did not die in winter.
fly verb
飛
常這樣用：fly + object/place
Birds can fly.
We fly a kite in the park.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A How are we getting to the beach for our trip?
B We can take the train at eight.
A Can we bring our bikes on it?
B Yes, but we need to ask at the station.
A My dad could drive us in his car instead.
B The road may have many trucks this morning.
A Then I will call him and choose the train.
B Great. Let's meet by the bicycle shop.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

My cousin Jack came from America during the school holiday. We planned a day trip to a mountain town and took an early train. At the station, Jack saw many bicycles and asked if we could rent a bike. Mom said the road was too steep, so we rode a bus instead. A truck drove slowly in front of us, but we reached the town before lunch. Jack took photos and called the trip his favorite day in Taiwan.

Emma was learning to ride a bicycle in the park. Dad held the bike while she pushed the pedals, but she turned too quickly and fell into the grass. She began to cry, so Dad asked if she was hurt. Emma shook her head and tried again. Soon she could ride past a parked truck without help. Above her, a red kite flew across the sky. Dad called Mom and said, "Emma can ride by herself now!"

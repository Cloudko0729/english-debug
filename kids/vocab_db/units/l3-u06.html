① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

America noun
美國
常這樣用：an america
My aunt lives in America.
America is a large country.
bicycle noun
腳踏車
常這樣用：a bicycle
The bicycle is near my home.
We can see the bicycle from here.
bike noun
腳踏車
常這樣用：a bike
The bike is near my home.
We can see the bike from here.
drive verb
開車
常這樣用：drive + object/place
My father drives to work.
Please drive slowly on this road.
into preposition
進入……裡面
常這樣用：Put the books into the box.
Put the books into the box.
The dog runs into the room.
train noun
火車
常這樣用：a train
We take the train to the city.
The train stops at this station.
trip noun
旅行
常這樣用：a trip
Our class trip is on Friday.
I packed a hat for the trip.
truck noun
卡車
常這樣用：a truck
The truck is near my home.
We can see the truck from here.
ask noun
問
常這樣用：an ask
I ask a question.
He asks for help.
call verb
打電話
常這樣用：call + object/place
I call my mom.
She calls her friend.
cry verb
哭
常這樣用：cry + object/place
The baby cries when she is hungry.
Do not cry; I can help you.
dance verb
跳舞
常這樣用：dance + object/place
I dance with my friends.
We dance in music class.
die verb
死亡
常這樣用：die + object/place
Plants die without water.
The old tree did not die in winter.
fly verb
飛
常這樣用：fly + object/place
Birds can fly.
We fly a kite in the park.

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

B No, but Uncle Sam has two bicycles.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

📖 有虛線的字是還沒學過的，點一下會出現中文。

My friend Sam came來（come 的過去式） from America for a family trip. Dad did not drive because we wanted to take a train to the sea. On the train, Sam saw看見（see 的過去式） a bird fly in the sky. When we got得到（get 的過去式） there, we saw看見（see 的過去式） a big truck at a store. Dad called his friend, and the man gave給（give 的過去式） us two bicycles. We had a great afternoon on the bikes.

Emma wanted to try her new bicycle after school. Dad helped her get on the bike, and she started to go. A truck came來（come 的過去式） by, so Dad asked her to wait. When it was gone不見了（go 的過去分詞）, Emma tried again. She went去（go 的過去式） too fast快的, and her bike went去（go 的過去式） into a tree. She did not cry because her arm and leg were fine. Dad called home and said說（say 的過去式）, "Emma can go now!"

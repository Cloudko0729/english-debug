① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

help verb
幫助
常這樣用：help + object/place
I help my dad.
She helps a friend.
invite verb
邀請、聘請
常這樣用：invite + object/place
I want to invite Amy to my party.
We invite our friends to the picnic.
join verb
加入
常這樣用：join + object/place
I want to join the music club.
Please join us for lunch.
kick verb
踢
常這樣用：kick + object/place
Do not kick the door.
He kicks the ball into the goal.
kiss verb
親吻
常這樣用：kiss + object/place
Mom gives the baby a kiss.
She kisses her child good night.
make verb
製作
常這樣用：make + object/place
I make a card.
He makes a cake.
need verb
需要
常這樣用：need + object/place
I need a pencil.
Plants need water and light.
sleep verb
睡覺
常這樣用：sleep + object/place
I sleep at night.
The baby sleeps well.
take verb
拿
常這樣用：take + object/place
I take my bag.
She takes a book.
try verb
嘗試
常這樣用：try + object/place
Please try this soup.
I try to read every day.
wait verb
等待
常這樣用：wait + object/place
Please wait for me.
We wait at the bus stop.
work verb
工作
常這樣用：work together
My parents work in the city.
We work together on the picture.
dry adjective
乾的
常這樣用：be dry
My socks are dry now.
Please dry the wet table.
autumn noun
秋天
常這樣用：an autumn
Autumn is cool.
Leaves fall in autumn.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A Do you want to join our soccer practice?
B Yes, but I need help with my kicks.
A I can work with you after school.
B Thanks. Should I invite Kevin too?
A Sure. We can make two small teams.
B Let's wait until the field is dry.
A Okay. I will take the ball home today.
B Great. We can try again tomorrow.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

Our school held an autumn fair on Saturday. I joined the art team because they needed help with the signs. We worked together to make bright posters and waited for the paint to dry. Then we invited families into the hall. Some children tried a ball game, and others learned a simple dance. I helped a small boy kick the ball toward a basket. He missed twice, but his third try went in, so everyone cheered.

We found a wet puppy near our gate after a rainy walk. My sister took it inside while I called a neighbor for help. We used a towel to dry its fur and tried to make it drink some water. The puppy needed a quiet place to sleep, so we put a box by the door and waited. Soon the owner arrived from work. She kissed the puppy, thanked us, and invited us to visit them again.

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

job / work / worker

job 是一份職業或工作；work 可指工作這件事或動詞；worker 是做工作的人。

My father has a new job.
My parents work in the city.
We have a lot of work today.
The worker fixes the road.
fall / autumn

作為季節時兩者都表示秋天；fall 較常見於美式英文，autumn 英美皆可用。fall 也可作動詞「掉落」。

Leaves fall in autumn.
We go hiking in the fall.
Autumn is cool.
Do not fall on the wet road.

我是： 🪙 —

① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

any determiner
任何；一些
常這樣用：Do you have any questions?
Do you have any questions?
I do not have any money.
dollar noun
元
常這樣用：a dollar
This pen is one dollar.
I have five dollars.
down adverb
向下；在下面
常這樣用：down
Please sit down.
The ball rolls down the road.
good-bye interjection
再見
常這樣用：a good-bye
Good-bye, Mr. Lin.
We say good-bye after class.
goodbye interjection
再見
常這樣用：a goodbye
Goodbye! See you tomorrow.
She waved goodbye to her friend.
money noun
錢
常這樣用：a money
I save my money.
There is some money in the bag.
oh-oh interjection
哦哦
常這樣用：an oh-oh
Oh-oh! My pencil is broken.
Oh-oh! We missed the bus.
oops interjection
噢！(等於whoops)
常這樣用：an oops
Oops! I dropped my book.
Oops! This is your cup.
really adverb
真的；非常
常這樣用：really
I really like this song.
Is that really your bag?
singing noun
(歌)唱、歌聲
常這樣用：a singing
Singing makes me happy.
We practice singing for the school show.
there adverb
那裡
常這樣用：there
The bus stop is over there.
There is a bird in the tree.
together adverb
一起
常這樣用：together
We study together.
Let us clean the room together.
uh-uh interjection
嗯嗯
常這樣用：an uh-uh
Uh-uh, that answer is not right.
Uh-uh, I do not want coffee.
up adverb
向上；在上面
常這樣用：up
Please stand up.
Look up at the sky.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A Oops, I dropped all the colored paper.
B Oh-oh! Some pieces went under the desk.
A Are there any more down there?
B Uh-uh, I think we found them all.
A Really? Then let's put them up on the shelf.
B We can carry the big box together.
A Thanks. I have to go now. Goodbye!
B Good-bye! See you at school tomorrow.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

Our English class made a pretend shop with play money. I put books on one table and wrote "one dollar" on each price card. Ben asked if we had any toy cars, so I looked under the table. "Oops, they are down here," I said. He was really happy and bought two. At the end, we counted the money together and packed everything up. We waved good-bye to our last customer and closed the shop.

The children were singing together for the school show. Amy stood up front, but she forgot one line and looked down at the floor. "Oh-oh," whispered Leo, but Amy remembered the words and kept going. The audience became quiet and listened. When the song ended, everyone clapped loudly. Amy smiled and said, "I really did it!" After the show, we picked up our bags, said goodbye to the teacher, and walked home together before dinner.

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

here / there：說話者的位置

here 是靠近說話者；there 是離說話者較遠的位置。

My bag is here.
The bus stop is over there.
Come here, please.
There is a bird in that tree.
some / any

肯定句常用 some；一般問句與否定句常用 any。邀請或預期答案是 yes 的問句也可用 some。

I need some paper.
Do you have any questions?
We do not have any milk.
Would you like some tea?

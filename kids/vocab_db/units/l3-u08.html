① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

any determiner
任何；一些
常這樣用：Do you have any questions?
Do you have any questions?
I do not have any money.
dollar noun
元
常這樣用：a dollar
This pen is one dollar.
I have five dollars.
down adverb
向下；在下面
常這樣用：down
Please sit down.
The ball rolls down the road.
good-bye interjection
再見
常這樣用：a good-bye
Good-bye, Mr. Lin.
We say good-bye after class.
goodbye interjection
再見
常這樣用：a goodbye

She waved goodbye to her friend.
money noun
錢
常這樣用：a money
I save my money.

oh-oh interjection
哦哦
常這樣用：an oh-oh

oops interjection
噢！(等於whoops)
常這樣用：an oops

really adverb
真的；非常
常這樣用：really

singing noun
(歌)唱、歌聲
常這樣用：a singing

there adverb

常這樣用：there

together adverb
一起
常這樣用：together

uh-uh interjection
嗯嗯
常這樣用：an uh-uh

up adverb
向上；在上面
常這樣用：up
Please stand up.
Look up at the sky.

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

here / there：說話者的位置

here 是靠近說話者；there 是離說話者較遠的位置。

My bag is here.
The bus stop is over there.
Come here, please.
There is a bird in that tree.
some / any

肯定句常用 some；一般問句與否定句常用 any。邀請或預期答案是 yes 的問句也可用 some。

I need some paper.
Do you have any questions?
We do not have any milk.
Would you like some tea?

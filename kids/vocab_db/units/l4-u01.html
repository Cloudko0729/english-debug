④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

am / is / are：主詞決定 be 動詞

I 配 am；he、she、it 和單數配 is；you、we、they 和複數配 are。情態動詞或 to 後使用 be。

I am ready.
The dog is under the desk.
They are good friends.
Please be quiet.
do / does：一般動詞問句

I、you、we、they 用 do；he、she、it 用 does。用了 does，後面的動詞回原形。

Do you like milk?
Does Amy walk to school?
We do our homework.
He does his homework.
this / that / these / those：距離與單複數

近處單數 this、遠處單數 that；近處複數 these、遠處複數 those。

This pencil is mine.
That bus is ours.
These books are new.
Those trees are tall.
say / speak / talk

say 重點是說出的內容；speak 常接語言或正式發言；talk 表示交談，常用 talk to/with/about。

Please say your name.
I can speak English.
I talk with my friend.
We talk about the movie.
and / but / or / so

and 加上相同方向資訊；but 表示轉折；or 提供選擇；so 表示前因後果。

I have a pen and a ruler.
The bag is old, but it is clean.
Do you want tea or water?
I was tired, so I went home.
some / any

肯定句常用 some；一般問句與否定句常用 any。邀請或預期答案是 yes 的問句也可用 some。

I need some paper.
Do you have any questions?
We do not have any milk.
Would you like some tea?

① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

grandfather noun
祖父；外祖父
常這樣用：a grandfather
My grandfather walks every morning.
I visit my grandfather on Sunday.
grandma noun
奶奶
常這樣用：a grandma
Grandma cooks soup for us.
My grandma smiles at me.
grandmother noun
祖母；外祖母
常這樣用：a grandmother
My grandmother makes good soup.
This card is for my grandmother.
grandpa noun
爺爺
常這樣用：a grandpa
Grandpa tells a funny story.
My grandpa drinks warm tea.
parents noun
父母
常這樣用：a parents
My parents love our family.
Parents help children learn.
sir title
先生
常這樣用：a sir
Yes, sir. I understand.
Excuse me, sir. Is this your bag?
writer noun
作家
常這樣用：a writer
The writer makes funny stories.
I want to be a writer.
tomorrow adverb
明天
常這樣用：tomorrow will
Tomorrow will be sunny.
I will visit Grandma tomorrow.
twelve number
十二
常這樣用：twelve + plural noun
There are twelve months in a year.
She has twelve cards.
twenty number
二十
常這樣用：twenty + plural noun
Twenty students read books.
I see twenty chairs.
weekend noun
週末
常這樣用：a weekend
The weekend is fun.
We visit grandma on the weekend.
butterfly noun
蝴蝶
常這樣用：a butterfly
I see a butterfly.
The butterfly is near the tree.
panda noun
熊貓
常這樣用：a panda
The panda is eating.
We saw a panda at the zoo.
thirsty adjective
渴的
常這樣用：be thirsty
I am thirsty.
She is thirsty after running.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A Are we visiting Grandpa this weekend?
B Yes. My parents want to go tomorrow morning.
A Will Grandma make lunch for us?
B She said she is making noodles at twelve.
A Great. Can we bring the panda puzzle?
B Sure, and Grandpa has a new butterfly book.
A I may get thirsty on the long bus ride.
B I will pack water for you and Grandmother.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

On Saturday, my parents took me to a butterfly garden with Grandma and Grandpa. A guide showed us a tiny egg under a leaf, and soon we saw a butterfly opening its wings. My grandmother took photos while my grandfather read the signs to me. At noon, we were thirsty, so we rested beside a pond and shared a bottle of water. A young panda was sleeping on a poster near the gift desk, and Grandma bought the card for my cousin. We stayed for almost two hours. On the way home, Grandpa said we should visit again next spring.

A children's writer visited our town on Sunday afternoon. My parents and I arrived at the reading hall before twelve, but every front seat was taken. The writer told a funny story about a panda that wanted to fly like a butterfly. My little brother laughed so hard that he dropped his bag. After the story, the writer answered twenty questions from the children. I asked how long it took to finish a book. "Sometimes a year," he said. Before we left, he wrote my name inside my book. I will show it to Grandma when we visit her tomorrow.

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

today / yesterday / tomorrow / ago

today 是今天；yesterday 是昨天；tomorrow 是明天；ago 從現在往前數一段時間。

Today is sunny.
Yesterday was rainy.
Tomorrow will be warm.
We met two days ago.

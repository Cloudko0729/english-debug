我是： 🪙 —

① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

alphabet noun
字母、字母表
常這樣用：an alphabet
We sing the alphabet song.
Write the alphabet from A to Z.
bookstore noun
書店
常這樣用：a bookstore
We buy books at the bookstore.
The bookstore is next to the bank.
crayon noun
有色粉筆、蠟筆、粉筆畫
常這樣用：a crayon
I color the sun with a yellow crayon.
My blue crayon is in the pencil box.
drawing noun
畫畫、圖畫、素描
常這樣用：a drawing
Her drawing is on the classroom wall.
I made a drawing of my family.
homework noun
家庭作業
常這樣用：a homework
I do my homework after school.
My math homework is on the desk.
page noun
頁
常這樣用：a page
Open your book to page ten.
There is a picture on this page.
paint verb
油畫
常這樣用：paint + object/place
I paint a red apple.
We paint pictures in class.
paper noun
紙
常這樣用：a paper
I write on a piece of paper.
We need some paper for the picture.
pencil box noun
鉛筆盒
常這樣用：a pencil box
My ruler is in the pencil box.
This pencil box is blue.
pencil case noun
鉛筆盒
常這樣用：a pencil case
Put your eraser in the pencil case.
Her pencil case is on the desk.
reading noun
閱讀、朗讀
常這樣用：a reading
Reading helps me learn new words.
We have reading time after lunch.
snake noun
蛇
常這樣用：a snake
I see a snake.
The snake is near the tree.
wind noun
風
常這樣用：a wind
The wind is strong today.
I can feel the wind on my face.
bread noun
麵包
常這樣用：bread
I eat bread for breakfast.
The bread is on the table.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A Did you see my pencil case in the bookstore?
B Is it the blue one with a snake drawing?
A Yes. My crayons and pencil are inside.
B I saw it next to the reading table.
A Was it under the alphabet book?
B No, it was on a page of brown paper.
A Thanks! I need it for my homework.
B Let's get it before the bookstore closes.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

After school, Mia went to the bookstore to finish her homework in the reading corner. She opened her pencil case and found one pencil, but her crayon box was missing. She remembered using it near the big window. As she walked back, a strong wind came through the open door and blew several pages across the floor. Mia helped the worker pick up the paper. Under a page with a snake drawing, she found her blue pencil box. Nothing was lost after all. She bought a small loaf of bread on the way home and finished her alphabet poster before dinner.

Leo's homework was to make an alphabet book, so he worked at a table outside his aunt's bakery. For the letter B, he painted a basket of bread. For S, he made a long green snake. He used a crayon for the grass and dark paint for the sky. The drawing looked strange, but he liked it. Then the wind lifted one page and carried it toward the road. Leo ran after the paper and caught it beside a parked scooter. He put every page into his pencil case for safety. That evening, his reading group enjoyed the colorful book.

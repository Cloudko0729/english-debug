我是： 🪙 —

① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

dish noun
盤子；一道菜
常這樣用：dish
Please put the salad in this dish.
I wash my dish after dinner.
feed verb
餵食
常這樣用：feed + object/place
Please feed the cat before school.
We feed the ducks at the park.
pork noun
豬肉
常這樣用：pork
Mom cooks pork for dinner.
The pork is in the dish.
salad noun
沙拉
常這樣用：salad
We have salad with dinner.
The salad has many vegetables.
snack noun
點心
常這樣用：snack
I eat fruit for my afternoon snack.
Please put your snack in the bag.
steak noun
牛排
常這樣用：steak
Dad cooks steak for dinner.
The steak is on the plate.
sugar noun
糖
常這樣用：sugar
I put a little sugar in my tea.
This cake has too much sugar.
sweet adjective
甜的
常這樣用：be sweet
The apple is sweet.
This tea is too sweet.
vegetable noun
蔬菜
常這樣用：vegetable
A carrot is a vegetable.
Please eat a vegetable with your dinner.
yucky adjective
令人厭惡的
常這樣用：be yucky
The old milk smells yucky.
This medicine tastes yucky.
yummy adjective
好吃的、美味的
常這樣用：be yummy
The cake is yummy.
This soup looks yummy.
balloon noun
氣球
常這樣用：a balloon
The red balloon is in the sky.
She has a balloon for the party.
bank noun
銀行
常這樣用：a bank
The bank is near the station.
My father visits the bank.
cellphone noun
手機
常這樣用：a cellphone
Mom calls me on her cellphone.
Please put your cellphone in your bag.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A Do you want pork or steak for dinner?
B I'll have the pork dish with a vegetable.
A Would you like salad with it?
B Yes, but please don't add the sweet sauce.
A Are you saving room for a snack?
B Maybe. The cake looks yummy.
A I think it has too much sugar.
B Then let's share one small piece.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

At lunch, our class tried a new meal in the school dining hall. The main dish had pork, rice, and a green vegetable. I usually leave vegetables on my plate because they look yucky, but Ben said the spinach was yummy. I took one bite and found it a little sweet. The salad also had corn, so I ate all of it. After lunch, we saved some clean leaves to feed the class rabbit. Our teacher gave us fruit for a snack and reminded us that animals should not eat sugar. I returned my empty dish feeling proud.

At the night market, I carried coins from my small bank in a yellow bag. Dad used his cellphone to check where our cousins were waiting. We met them beside a balloon stand, and then everyone chose a snack. My cousin ordered grilled pork, while I picked a vegetable roll. It smelled wonderful, but the first bite was too hot. Later, we shared a sweet pancake with less sugar. Dad wanted steak, but the line was very long, so he bought a cold salad instead. Before going home, I paid for a blue balloon and tied it tightly to my bag.

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

phone / telephone / cellphone

phone 是最常用總稱；telephone 可指電話或較正式說法；cellphone 特別指行動電話。

I call Mom on the phone.
What is your telephone number?
My cellphone is in my bag.
The office telephone is on the desk.

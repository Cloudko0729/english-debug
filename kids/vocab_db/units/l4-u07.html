① 先認識這些字

點一下卡片會翻開中文和例句。先自己猜猜看是什麼意思，再翻開對答案。

buy verb
買
常這樣用：buy + object/place
I buy a pencil.
She buys milk.
dig verb
挖
常這樣用：dig + object/place
The dog likes to dig in the sand.
We dig a small hole for the tree.
fall verb
秋天；掉落
常這樣用：fall + object/place
Leaves fall from the tree.
Fall is another word for autumn.
pay verb
付錢
常這樣用：pay + object/place
I pay for lunch.
He pays ten dollars.
send verb
寄送
常這樣用：send + object/place
Please send this card to Grandma.
I send a message to my friend.
stay verb
停留
常這樣用：stay + object/place
Please stay here with me.
We stay inside when it rains.
stop verb
停止；車站
常這樣用：stop + object/place
Please stop at the red light.
The rain will stop soon.
swim verb
游泳
常這樣用：swim + object/place
I swim in the pool.
We swim on warm days.
use verb
使用
常這樣用：use + object/place
I use a ruler to draw a line.
Can I use your blue crayon?
worry verb
擔心
常這樣用：worry + object/place
Do not worry; I can help you.
I worry when my dog is missing.
street noun
街道
常這樣用：a street
Our school is on this street.
Look both ways before you cross the street.
visit verb
拜訪；參觀
常這樣用：visit + object/place
I'm going to visit my uncle.
We will visit the old town soon.
best adjective
最好的(adj) 最好地
常這樣用：be best
This is my best picture.
Amy is my best friend.
better adjective
較好的、更好的(健康)好轉的(adj) 更好地
常這樣用：be better
I feel better today.
This plan is better than the old one.

② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

A Can I stop at the shallow end today?
B Sure. You don't need to swim far.
A I fell yesterday, so I still worry.
B Use the side rail until you feel better.
A Should I stay close to you?
B Yes, and take your time.
A I'll try my best on the next lap.
B Good. Call me when you reach the end.

③ 讀一段短文

同樣這些字，放在一個完整的故事裡。可以邊聽邊看。

During our beach visit, Mia and I wanted to build the best sandcastle on the shore. We used small cups to dig a deep wall, but one side began to fall. I started to worry because the tide was coming in. Mia said we should stop and move farther from the water. Our second castle was smaller, but it stayed strong. After finishing it, we went to swim with Dad and stayed inside the safe area. On the way home, Dad let us buy cold drinks. The first castle looked bigger, but we agreed the second plan was better.

Ben wanted to send a birthday card to his cousin, so he visited the post office after school. He wrote the address carefully but forgot to add the street number. A worker noticed the problem before Ben paid for the stamp. "Do not worry," she said. Ben called his mother and used her answer to fix the address. Then he chose the best stamp, paid at the counter, and put the card in the box. Outside, rain started to fall, so he stayed under the roof for a few minutes. When it stopped, he walked home feeling much better.

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

fall / autumn

作為季節時兩者都表示秋天；fall 較常見於美式英文，autumn 英美皆可用。fall 也可作動詞「掉落」。

Leaves fall in autumn.
We go hiking in the fall.
Autumn is cool.
Do not fall on the wet road.

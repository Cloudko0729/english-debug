② 聽聽看什麼時候會用到

這些字在真的講話時長這樣。先整段聽一次，再一句一句聽。

B Yes, but it is cool under these trees.
A Your new dress is very pretty.
B Thank you. My sister has the same one.
A Is she the tall one with long hair?
B Yes, and she is very smart and funny.
A Are you sure she is only twelve?

④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

big / small / tall / long / short

big/small 說整體大小；tall 說由下到上的高度；long/short 說長度，short 也可說身高較矮。

The elephant is big.
The ant is small.
The tree is tall.
The road is long.
hot / warm / cool

hot 是很熱；warm 是舒適的溫暖；cool 是稍冷或涼爽。

The soup is hot.
The tea is warm.
The evening air is cool.
Summer days are hot.

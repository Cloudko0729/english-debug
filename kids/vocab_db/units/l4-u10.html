④ 這幾組最容易搞混

看清楚差在哪裡，之後就不會用錯。

under / in front of / next to

under 是正下方；in front of 是前方；next to 是緊鄰旁邊。

The cat is under the chair.
The bus is in front of the school.
The bank is next to the bookstore.
My shoes are under the bed.
sport / sports / swimming / soccer

sport 可指一種運動；sports 指運動活動的總稱；swimming 與 soccer 是具體運動名稱。

Soccer is a team sport.
We play sports after school.
I go swimming on Sunday.
My brother plays soccer.

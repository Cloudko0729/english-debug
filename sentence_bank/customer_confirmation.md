# Customer Confirmation Sentence Bank

客戶確認、驗證、回覆相關的實用英文句型庫。

---

## 請客戶確認

- Please confirm the test result on your end.
- Could you please verify the unit and let us know the result?
- Kindly confirm once you have completed the verification.

## 等待客戶回覆

- We are waiting for your confirmation.
- Please let us know if the issue can be reproduced on your side.
- We will proceed once we receive your confirmation.

## 客戶回覆後的動作

- Thank you for your confirmation. We will arrange the shipment accordingly.
- Based on your feedback, we will proceed with the next steps.

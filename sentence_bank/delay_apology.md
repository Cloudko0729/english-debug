# Delay Apology and Revised ETA

專業說明延誤原因、更新預計完成時間（ETA），維持客戶信任。

---

## 告知延誤

- We would like to inform you that there will be a delay in [item/process].
- Unfortunately, the [repair / analysis / delivery] is taking longer than expected.
- We apologize for the delay and would like to provide an update.
- Due to [reason], we are unable to meet the original schedule.

## 說明延誤原因

- The delay is due to additional testing required to confirm the root cause.
- We encountered an unexpected issue during the repair process that requires further investigation.
- The analysis is taking longer than expected because we need to run additional tests.
- The replacement parts have not yet arrived, which has caused a delay in the repair timeline.

## 提供更新的 ETA

- We now expect to complete the repair by [date].
- The revised estimated delivery date is [date].
- We anticipate being able to ship the unit by [date] at the latest.
- We will provide a further update if the timeline changes.

## 道歉 + 承諾跟進

- We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused.
- We understand this delay may affect your schedule and apologize for the disruption.
- We are doing our best to resolve this as quickly as possible.
- We will keep you informed of any developments and update you immediately once the issue is resolved.

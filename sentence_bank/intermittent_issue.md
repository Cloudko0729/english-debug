# Intermittent Issue Description

描述偶發問題、觸發條件、頻率與重現步驟，適合技術除錯與客戶回報。

---

## 描述偶發性

- The issue occurs intermittently and cannot be reproduced consistently.
- The failure appears to be intermittent — it does not occur every time.
- The issue occurs intermittently when [condition].
- We have been unable to reproduce the issue consistently under standard test conditions.

## 描述觸發條件

- The issue is more likely to occur under high-temperature conditions.
- The failure tends to appear after the unit has been running for several hours.
- The issue seems to be triggered by [specific signal / condition / sequence].
- The problem only appears when [module A] is connected to [module B].

## 描述頻率

- The issue occurs approximately once every [X] hours / [X] cycles.
- The failure has been observed [X] times out of [Y] test runs.
- The problem is sporadic — we have seen it happen maybe once or twice during extended testing.

## 重現步驟 / 縮小範圍

- We are currently trying to narrow down the trigger condition.
- To reproduce the issue, please follow these steps: [steps].
- We have isolated the problem to [component / condition] based on the current test results.
- Further testing is needed to confirm whether the issue is related to [factor A] or [factor B].

# Part Number and Spec Confirmation

確認料號、型號、版本、替代料與相容性，適合維修與採購溝通。

---

## 確認料號 / 型號

- Could you confirm the exact part number and model version?
- Please provide the full part number as shown on the label.
- Could you double-check the part number? We want to make sure we order the correct replacement.
- The part number on the label appears to be [XXX]. Could you verify this?

## 確認規格 / 版本

- Could you confirm which hardware revision this unit is?
- Please confirm the firmware version currently installed on the unit.
- We need to verify the connector specification before selecting the replacement part.
- Could you provide the datasheet or spec sheet for this component?

## 替代料 / 相容性確認

- To avoid using the wrong replacement part, we need to verify the connector specification.
- Is [part number] compatible with the current hardware revision?
- Could you confirm whether [part A] is a direct replacement for [part B]?
- We need to confirm that the substitute part meets the original specification before proceeding.

## 請提供標籤照片

- Could you provide a photo of the full model number and serial number on the label?
- Please take a photo of the label on the bottom of the unit and send it to us.
- A clear photo of the part number label would help us confirm the correct replacement.

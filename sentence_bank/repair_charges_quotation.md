# Repair Charges and Quotation Sentence Bank

說明保固外維修費用、報價、客戶確認與付款後續的常用句。
---

## 說明保固狀態

- This unit is out of warranty, so a repair charge will apply.
- The warranty period has expired for this unit.
- Based on the purchase date and serial number, this repair is not covered under warranty.
- We will provide a quotation before proceeding with the repair.

## 說明費用內容

- The repair charge includes inspection, replacement parts, labor, and final testing.
- The quotation covers the replacement part and the repair service fee.
- Additional charges may apply if further damage is found during inspection.
- No repair work will be performed until we receive your approval.

## 請客戶確認報價

- Please review the attached quotation and confirm whether we may proceed.
- Kindly confirm your approval of the repair charge so we can continue the repair process.
- Once we receive your approval, we will arrange the repair accordingly.
- If the quotation is not approved, we can return the unit as-is.

## 說明後續流程

- After payment is confirmed, we will proceed with the repair.
- The estimated repair lead time is [X] business days after quotation approval.
- We will update you once the repair is completed and the unit passes final testing.
- Please let us know if you need the quotation revised with your billing information.

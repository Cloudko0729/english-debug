# Schedule and Delivery Sentence Bank

交期、出貨、時程相關的實用英文句型庫。

---

## 確認交期

- Could you please confirm the estimated delivery date?
- Please advise on the expected shipment date.
- When is the earliest possible delivery date?

## 通知出貨

- The unit has been shipped.
- The shipment has been arranged.
- We will ship the unit once the verification is complete.
- The estimated delivery date is [date].

## 延誤說明

- There will be a slight delay due to [reason].
- We apologize for the delay and will update you as soon as possible.
- The shipment has been postponed to [date] due to [reason].

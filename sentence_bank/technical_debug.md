# Technical Debug Sentence Bank

技術問題描述、debug 報告相關的實用英文句型庫。

---

## 問題描述

- The issue occurs only under specific conditions.
- This behavior was observed intermittently.
- The anomaly appears to be related to the EEPROM data mismatch.
- We suspect the issue may be caused by the copied board.

## 根本原因分析

- The root cause has not been identified yet.
- We are still investigating the root cause.
- Based on our analysis, the issue may be related to...
- A checksum mismatch was found during the comparison.

## 比對與驗證

- We need to compare the golden file one more time.
- The firmware version was confirmed to be correct.
- No difference was found between the two units.
- The data on the copied board does not match the original.

## 無法重現問題

- We were unable to reproduce the issue on our side.
- The issue could not be reproduced under the same conditions.
- No abnormal behavior was observed during our testing.

## 索取除錯資訊

- Could you please provide the log file for further analysis?
- Could you please confirm which firmware version was used during the test?
- It would be helpful if you could share a photo of the error message.
- Could you please provide us with the log file and the firmware version?
- To reproduce the issue, we would also need the test steps and the configuration used.
